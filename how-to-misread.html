How to misread the Well

Read evidence classes before conclusions. A relationship is a question, not a coupling. A frozen plan is a proposal, not a result. A hash identifies bytes relative to a reference, not truth, authority, safety or a trusted time. A local acknowledgement is not approval.

Evidence ladder

  1. Editorial comparison or guided hypothesis: a research direction.
  2. Proposed test plan: acceptance criteria, not executed evidence.
  3. Same-author determinism check: a finite set of cases matches authored expectations. A replay, including on another platform, does not independently validate the model.
  4. Independent implementation or analysis: separate reviewer and declared method; distinguish blind reimplementation from review of existing code.
  5. Independent data or physical test: a separate evidence class requiring its own protocol, uncertainty, limitations and safety review.

Negative controls are deliberately chosen test cases, not necessarily observed experimental failures. Retain every planned case, contrary result, execution error and exclusion with its reason. Missing data is not zero. A result does not automatically move a design into a stronger evidence class.

Private drafting stays offline; explicit archives are readable files, not encrypted storage. Keep them out of public repositories. The Chronicle preserves observed runs separately from the proposed queue. No independent replication is claimed for the current synthetic fixtures.
