Where does the field energy go?

The design review narrowed a field-energy analogy to a question about spectral coupling and ion motion.

Model proposed

What we intended

Model energy transfer from a prescribed electromagnetic field into a confined ion.

What the source documents

The revised proposal distinguishes electronic excitation from centre-of-mass motion and specifies spectral overlap as a condition for excitation. No FQSC-01 simulation output or laboratory measurement is reported.

Possible crossover

A weak-field model could teach how two field histories with the same energy produce different internal and motional responses.

Next test

Compare resonant and detuned weak-field histories with matched energy. Record state populations and motional energy separately.
