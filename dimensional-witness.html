Dimensional Witness

Version 2026-11-25-v2. A conservative review gate for explicit symbol mappings. Not an algebra engine, solver, unit converter, or expression-level dimensional checker. Matching dimensions never establish physical equivalence.

Open Equation Bench | Annotated source definitions | Machine-readable annotations.

What the gate does

  • Known incompatible physical dimensions and Boolean/numeric mismatches block a same-quantity claim.
  • Matching dimensions, missing unit scales, differing units, unknown dimensions and matching Boolean types require a written explanation and explicit user acknowledgement before a same-quantity claim can be exported.
  • Different-quantity choices remain possible without declaring a physical relationship. Editing the target, decision or notes revokes that row acknowledgement. Changing annotation version requires fresh review.
  • Unit labels and quantity kinds are reviewed annotations of the source definitions, not newly measured data. Unspecified units remain unspecified. The exponent order is mass, length, time, thermodynamic temperature (M,L,T,Theta); null means not established or not applicable. Angles, counts, fractions and percentages are dimensionless in the SI sense but carry different meanings; Boolean values are not physical quantities.
  • A same-quantity claim is a user hypothesis even after acknowledgement. The Witness does not validate meaning, clocks, reference frames, sampling, scale conversions, vector/tensor shape or the final expression.

Outcome and review matrix

Reviewed, not approved. No acknowledgement can override a hard refusal.
