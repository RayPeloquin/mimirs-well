An evidence-first collection

Not every project needs a divergence.

The Well's main project registry remains the complete inventory. A Divergence Ledger entry is curated when there is a meaningful documented change, test, contradiction, revised assumption or alternative research question. No automatic one-to-one conversion from project cards to ledger entries.

Statuses are evidence descriptions, not maturity scores. A model is not physical validation, a Court critique is not an experiment and a possible crossover is a hypothesis until tested. Record numbers never change; actual update dates can.
