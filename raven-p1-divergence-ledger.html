01 / The three questions

RAVEN-P1 · Divergence Record

01 / INTENDED

What we intended

Generate and rank candidate sequences against the published 92-residue Top7 structural scaffold, inspect predicted geometry, and preserve mutation, sequence and model provenance.

The saved v0.1 summary reports 64 distinct sequence generations and selects a 92-residue sequence containing 50 substitutions relative to the reference.

02 / OBSERVED

What actually happened

The candidate's saved ProteinMPNN mean negative log-likelihood was 0.8895 versus 1.6676 for the reference under the reported scoring procedure. A saved OmegaFold prediction placed its C-alpha backbone approximately 0.781 Å RMSD from the Top7 template. These are model-dependent predictions, not measured folding, stability or function.

The candidate was selected with the same model family used for generation, and Top7 may overlap training material. The repository's README says the full original reproduction pack remains incomplete.

03 / CROSSOVER

What else might this be useful for?

Its most useful near-term transfer may be a provenance and model-sensitivity workflow: preserve intermediate candidates, pose mutation-order controls, compare predictors and detect where a favored model is simply rewarding its own preferences.

A proposed research-quality method, not a new therapeutic or biochemical result.

The divergence: Model agreement can justify the next falsifiable experiment. It cannot replace synthesis, folding measurements or independent prediction.

03 / Next reproducible brick

How to test the next question

1 · Restore reproducibility

Publish or securely preserve the missing v0.1 scripts, dependencies, checkpoints and referenced structure outputs with hashes.

2 · Try truly independent models

Run unrelated predictors and sequence-scoring controls with held-out selection rules, and report sensitivity to seeds and templates.

3 · Separate biology from computation

Only a qualified laboratory program could test expression, structure, solubility, folding and safety. No therapeutic application is claimed.
