Which joules cross the boundary twice?

Separate lamp and receiver balances clarify how to count light transferred inside the apparatus.

Calculation-only design

What we intended

Compare how adjustable light-collection panels route energy to a receiver and load.

What the source documents

The design note now separates lamp input from light incident on the receiver. Its 10 J incident and 2 J delivered example illustrates 20% receiver efficiency. Those values are hypothetical, and the whole-apparatus efficiency remains unknown.

Possible crossover

The two balances could be used to audit other optical test rigs where an intermediate reading is mistaken for an additional output.

Next test
