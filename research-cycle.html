The research cycle

Start anywhere, or follow this ordered reading route. The stages are a human-reviewed workflow, not an automatic promotion system. No stage authorizes physical testing or publication.

  1. The Scriptorium

    Capture unfinished ideas privately; export only deliberately separated review material.

  2. The Orrery

    Explore curated relationships and their limitations; no inferred compatibility.

  3. Guided Alchemy

    Choose a proposed research question, not an established coupling.

  4. Dimensional Witness

    Review quantity meanings and refuse incompatible mappings; no approval of equations.

  5. The Alchemist's Equation Bench

    Prepare a bounded mathematical hypothesis and test brief; the separate interactive workspace is also available. Open the interactive Alchemist's Bench.

  6. The Crucible Work Queue

    Specify a baseline, fixture, acceptance, contrary and stop conditions before execution.

  7. The Chronicle

    Preserve observed outcomes, inputs, source hashes, contrary controls and reproduction limits.

  8. The Divergence Ledger

    Record evidence and unresolved questions; return to the Scriptorium to revise.

Read the limits first

How to misread the Well: evidence classes, hashes, privacy, and the distinction between a deterministic check and independent science. Publication safeguards remain in force. No independent replication is claimed by the current Chronicle fixtures.
