01 / The three questions

Keep intent, evidence and hypothesis apart.

01 / INTENDED

What we intended

Explore a live reporter of one host-cell state and a separate one-bit DNA exposure mark, with an external observation ledger that retains the specific cell or cohort and readout window.

A goal is not an experimental result.

02 / DOCUMENTED

What actually happened

The v0.4 public design separates recorded, qualified blank and unknown outcomes. No integrated construct, host-genome separation measurement or recording performance is reported. A one-bit latch cannot give an exact event time or count; missing, silenced or lost cells cannot be silently scored as negative.

This describes the available source record; no new tests are implied.

03 / POSSIBLE CROSSOVER

What else might this be useful for?

The three-way evidence model may also improve ordinary event logging: distinguish evidence of absence from missing evidence, failed sensors and unreadable history. This remains a proposed crossover, not a biological discovery.

New hypothesis only; independent validation needed.

Evidence boundary: A qualified blank can still miss exposure. The external ledger is not a cellular safety governor.
02 / Next reproducible brick

What would change the record?

2 · Measure

3 · Challenge

Test location, integration detection limits, stability and losses separately.
