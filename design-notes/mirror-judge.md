# Mirror Judge

**Status:** Archived research concept; not independently validated or certified.

## Purpose and proposed mechanism
A proposed output-integrity gate using impact, role reversal and accountability tests to challenge manipulative or unjustifiable advice.


This concept concerns evaluation of advice and its impact, not execution permission. See also [related archive note](conduct-architecture.md).

Example to test: persuasive advice applies inconsistent standards when the roles of affected parties are reversed. This is a proposed failure case, not an observed result.

## Evidence limits
This is a concise public interpretation of a privately held desktop design note, not its complete technical specification. Claims of readiness, resilience, safety, accuracy or performance in the source have not been independently verified. No field trial, formal proof or regulatory approval is asserted here.
