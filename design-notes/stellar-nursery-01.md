# Stellar Nursery-01

**Status:** v1.1 computed gas-disk model; no observational fit or independent astrophysical validation.

## Purpose
Model the later assembly of a young single-star system **after a protostar already exists**. A finite gas envelope feeds a rotating disk; prescribed transport and a thermal calculation track mass and angular momentum subject to stated assumptions.

## What was calculated
The archived mathematical package reports a worked numerical example, conservative ledgers and **28/28 internal numerical checks across three spatial resolutions**. Those checks assess the implementation under its chosen assumptions. They do not confirm that the modeled disk matches a real star system.

## Evidence limits
No simulation of initial molecular-cloud collapse, planet formation, spiral-arm physics, nonideal magnetic effects or named astronomical observations. Numerical consistency is not a new physical law or an observed young star.

## Next check
Release a reproducible benchmark with initial conditions, solver and conservation tolerances; compare against independent disk models and publicly measured disk observables before physical interpretation.
