# Ocean Boundary Residual Inference (OBRI)

**Status:** v0.2 proposed detection-theory extension; not adopted as canon; no hardware.

## Purpose
Investigate whether an additional electromagnetic environmental observation could improve a **scientific uncertainty model** for unresolved boundaries in seawater relative to a hydrodynamics-only baseline. Ordinary conductive-fluid physics underlies the question; the hypothetical improvement belongs to the proposed inference method.

## Boundary of the claim
The archived state block states that motional induction and related oceanographic principles are established background science. It does **not** establish that every underwater object has a usable signal or that any particular target can be located. This public digest excludes tactical deployment parameters and platform-specific tracking methodology.

## Evidence limits
No measured inference gain, actual boundary detection, quantified operational range, field trial, verified discrimination or demonstrated integration. The v0.2 revision was explicitly pending Court review, not a completed validation or release.

## Next check
Define a safe, non-operational synthetic conductivity/flow dataset with blind labels; compare the extra channel with a baseline and report uncertainty, false positives and sensitivity to model misspecification.
