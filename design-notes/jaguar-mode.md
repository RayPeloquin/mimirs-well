# Jaguar Mode — Low-disturbance robotics

**Status:** v1.1 platform-agnostic behavioral proposal; no measured integration.

## Purpose
Explore a human-friendly robot behavior that favors smooth, predictable movement over speed or dramatic motion when sharing space with people or animals. The archive explicitly describes a **non-combat, non-pursuit** mode.

## Design direction
Proposed principles include conservative acceleration, yielding on unexpected contact, readable intentions, operator control and a safe halt when perception or stability becomes uncertain. Its interface to a particular robot's certified safety system remains unspecified.

## Evidence limits
No measured noise reduction, validated contact-force threshold, user comfort study, platform trial or proven safety certification. Percentages in the archive are candidate settings, not universal safety limits. Reduced disturbance must not become covert tracking.

## Next check
Choose a benign mobility platform and independently verified safety envelope; compare human perceptions and emergency-stop response across baseline and candidate motion policies with operator oversight.
