# BAMBOO-RF — Agricultural phenotype and material research

**Status:** Phase 1 visual protocol overview; no measured study or functional RF prototype.

## Purpose
Explore whether measurable differences among bamboo growth forms, fiber orientations and moisture states can inform later material selection and characterization. A useful first result would be an **honest specimen dataset** linking plant phenotype and controlled dielectric measurements, not a working antenna or genetically engineered crop.

## Archive evidence
The saved public-facing concept image is labeled **BAMBOO-RF Phase 1: Natural Phenotype Survey**. It sketches sampling, specimen labeling, geometry measurements, environmental context and basic laboratory characterization. The catalog also calls this an *agricultural tolerance concept*, but the image does not demonstrate drought, pest or stress tolerance.

## Evidence limits
No harvested specimen record, calibrated dataset, demonstrated RF structure, enhanced crop tolerance or field performance. The depicted later phases—including material conversion or functionalization—were **not** part of the Phase 1 evidence and are not validated.

## Next check
Write a benign, independently reviewable plant-sampling and data dictionary first; establish controls for species, moisture, growth conditions and repeat measurements before considering material-device claims.
