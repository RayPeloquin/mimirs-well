# Asteris Daydream Loop

**Status:** Proposed bounded research-workflow design; no background process is running.

## Purpose
Explore scheduled or explicitly requested short research sessions that select one approved open question, consider a small number of ideas, check assumptions and save a concise journal entry. The word **daydream** describes a workflow, not evidence of subjective experience.

## Governance design
Use a bounded memory shelf with source attribution, fixed session budgets, explicit stop rules and a distinct delivery gate. A loop must not silently increase its permissions, spend, runtime or outreach authority. It may record “nothing useful” instead of amplifying a weak idea.

## Evidence limits
No running scheduler, persistent autonomous research service, demonstrated quality improvement or evidence of machine consciousness. Repetition of a speculation does not upgrade it to a fact; a useful hypothesis needs an independent falsification condition.

## Next check
Implement a local, read-only mock session with synthetic seeds, fixed limits, provenance labels and a separate human approval gate; test how false claims are rejected and how the process stops.
