# Endurance Stack

**Status:** Archived research concept; not independently validated or certified.

## Purpose and proposed mechanism
Energy and thermal budget management for remote platforms, linking HIPPO power arbitration, CalmStack attention filtering and operator-defined priorities. Onboard reserve checks remain authoritative.


This concept concerns resource budgets rather than general measurement validity. See also [related archive note](system-validity-platform.md).

Example to test: a simulated battery reserve estimate becomes stale during communication loss. This is a proposed failure case, not an observed result.

## Evidence limits
This is a concise public interpretation of a privately held desktop design note, not its complete technical specification. Claims of readiness, resilience, safety, accuracy or performance in the source have not been independently verified. No field trial, formal proof or regulatory approval is asserted here.

## Proposed next verification check
Start with a non-flight battery simulator and test thermal derating, lost communications and return-reserve handling.
