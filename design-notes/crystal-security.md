# Crystal Security — Passive disturbance witness

**Status:** Crystal Sentinel Security Node v0.2 concept; not experimentally validated.

## Purpose
Explore a low-power, privacy-respecting disturbance indicator using a passive vibration witness and optional independent non-imaging confirmation. Its purpose is to signal that a physical change **may** need human attention, not to identify a person or determine intent.

## Proposed boundaries
Document environmental conditions, keep sensing separate from interpretation, and avoid imaging or identity inference. Related archive names such as OilSkin, HEX-POL and CalmStack are possible interfaces, not evidence of integrated hardware.

## Evidence limits
No measured range, battery life, environmental robustness, false-alarm rate or independent security evaluation. Component selections and numerical targets in the archive have not been substantiated by trials.

## Next check
Specify a benign bench protocol using ordinary environmental motion and control conditions; measure repeatability and false alarms with transparent privacy constraints.
