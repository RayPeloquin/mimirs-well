# Consent-Gated Interpretation

**Status:** Archived research concept; not independently validated or certified.

## Purpose and proposed mechanism
An interaction pattern that separates analysis from permission to interrupt. Proposed output requires presence, physical confirmation and confidence filtering; absent consent defaults to silence.


This concept concerns permission to present an interpretation, rather than general execution authority. See also [related archive note](conduct-architecture.md).

Example to test: consent is withdrawn while an analysis is still running. This is a proposed failure case, not an observed result.

## Evidence limits
This is a concise public interpretation of a privately held desktop design note, not its complete technical specification. Claims of readiness, resilience, safety, accuracy or performance in the source have not been independently verified. No field trial, formal proof or regulatory approval is asserted here.

## Proposed next verification check
Test withdrawal of consent, data-retention controls, emergency exceptions and output suppression.
