# Quantum Sovereign Stack

**Status:** v1.0C Phase 0 architecture with a simulation charter; untested integration.

## Purpose
Define an auditable **classical control and evidence layer around quantum hardware**: health reports, calibration-age checks, workload scheduling, resource gates, fallback, and human escalation. It does not claim to build a new quantum computer or control wavefunctions.

## Proposed composition
The archived state block calls this an infrastructure *cockpit*, coordinating narrower functions such as observation, scheduling, gatekeeping and maintenance. It lists an evidence level and internal readiness gate, but those are **archive governance labels**, not external certification. Related Quantum Shepherd and Quantum Floor designs may supply modules; their exact boundaries remain to be specified.

## Evidence limits
No operational quantum integration, measured circuit success improvement, complete simulation score, independently validated resource efficiency or external review certification. “Build-ready” in the source means prepared for a scoped simulation, not field-proven.

## Next check
Implement a deterministic simulated workload and telemetry replay, compare against a declared baseline, then measure unsafe admissions, missed work, latency, calibration freshness and operator burden.
