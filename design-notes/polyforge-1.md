# Polyforge-1 — Ocean cleanup ship

**Status:** Archived concept image only; no demonstrated prototype.

## Public concept

An illustrated slow-speed marine-debris collection vessel that would receive larger floating plastic and net material for onboard handling.

The artwork depicts guiding booms, a collection mouth, controlled handling and dewatering, and a proposed downstream plastics stream including polymer-pellet storage.

## Evidence limits

No designed or launched ship, sea trial, mass balance, sorting purity, recycling yield, energy budget, wildlife-risk study, maritime clearance, or verified certification is available. Labels about processing outputs do not establish a saleable recycled material.
