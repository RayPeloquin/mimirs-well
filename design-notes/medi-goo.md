# Medi Goo — Soft surgical interface concept

**Status:** Speculative material architecture; no preclinical or clinical validation.

## Purpose
Investigate a temporary, compliant interface concept intended to distribute contact pressure and separate delicate tissue from a device surface under trained human control. This is a research direction, **not** a ready-to-use medical product or material recipe.

## Lineage note
The catalog says **Medi Goo**; the archived `medi goo.txt` contains a heading for **MIMIR'S WELL-M v1.0 Artemis Series Adaptive Surgical Interface Material Architecture**. Equivalence of names and version lineage remains uncertain and should not be silently assumed.

## Safety concept
Keep any behavior passive-safe and manually supervised. Material identity, adhesion, retrieval, residue, cleaning, tissue response and infection-control requirements all remain unresolved.

## Evidence limits
No characterized material formulation, sterility data, biocompatibility testing, bench validation, clinical assessment or human-use approval. Do not use this description for treatment or device construction.
