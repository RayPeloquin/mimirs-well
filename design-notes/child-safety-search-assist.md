# Child Safety Search Assist

**Status:** v0.1 theoretical proposal and review; no deployed service or child-data collection.

## Purpose
Explore an **authorized, human-reviewed public-information assistant** for finding and checking updates about existing missing-person cases. The design separates case-information organization from recognizing a potential request for help; success in either task would not prove that disappearances were prevented.

## Proposed boundaries
Use specifically permitted public sources, minimize retention, preserve provenance and uncertainty, and leave verification and contact with responsible organizations to trained humans. No sweeping collection from nearby electronics, covert surveillance, device intrusion, automated accusation or unsolicited public identification.

## Evidence limits
No software, live monitoring, measured precision or recall, agency partnership, operational rescue capability or child-safety outcome has been demonstrated. Headline incident statistics from different reporting systems must not be conflated.

## Next check
Choose one lawful, tightly scoped public-source task, obtain safeguarding and privacy review, define false-identification harms and escalation rules, then evaluate synthetic examples before any real-case consideration.
