# VEIL-E / EDISON-01 — The Quiet Receiver

**Status:** v3.0 research concept dated 29 October 2026; not built or calibrated, with no anomalous signal or contact result. Public summary prepared 19 November 2026.

## Purpose

Design a controlled weak-force experiment to distinguish reproducible physical measurements from instrument artifacts. The question of information-bearing signals is deliberately separate from identifying any proposed communicator.

## Proposed boundary

- Start with two matched, sealed torsion balances and independent optical readouts. Preserve both raw sensor channels, synchronized environmental measurements and the analysis plan.
- Calibrate with independently randomized known forces and shams; investigate optical heating, vibration, electrostatics, temperature, magnetism and instrument drift.
- Keep data acquisition free from generated voices, faces or suggested semantic interpretations.
- Only after independent replication of a physically unexplained effect could a separately designed, blinded challenge-response experiment address information transfer. A residual is not evidence of a person, spirit or deceased identity.

## Evidence and limits

The Court's document provides an engineering comparison, idealized measurement equations and proposed experiment gates. Those are not measured performance figures. No physical coupling from consciousness or a deceased person to torsion has been established. Null outcomes are valid and constrain only the defined apparatus and exposure; they do not settle every possible hypothesis.

## Next check

Define a measurable input-force range, choose a benchtop sensor arrangement, establish known-input recovery and an honest false-candidate rate, then pre-register artifact and null controls before any unusual-signal interpretation.
