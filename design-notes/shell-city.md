# Shell City — Inverted-mushroom barn

**Status:** Archived concept image only; no demonstrated prototype.

## Public concept

A speculative below-grade farm and shelter design exploring shell-shaped enclosures, mushroom cultivation and a shared agricultural space.

The concept images explore interlocking or inverted-shell geometry and an underground cultivation-and-barn arrangement. The built environment is rendered as an imaginative spatial proposal.

## Archived visual reference

Shell City’s underground mushroom farm and barn.png; Twin spiral shell cave city.png. The original image material remains in the private archive; this text is a source-limited public digest.

## Evidence limits

No site survey, geology, structural calculation, daylight or ventilation design, drainage and waterproofing study, fire egress, food-growing yield, livestock welfare case, budget or permit review is recorded.

## Next check

Separate the architectural ideas into a program and safe schematic; ask qualified specialists to evaluate subsurface ground, air, water and evacuation constraints for a real site.
