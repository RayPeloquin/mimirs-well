# EMH FIELD — Guide Gloves

**Status:** v0.1 requirements candidate dated 10 October 2026; no clinical deployment or prototype. Public summary prepared 19 November 2026.

## Purpose

Study a portable, human-controlled medical guidance interface. A trained person performs the actual task; a camera and lightweight guide gloves would supply selected posture sensing and small tactile cues. The proposed EMH display could explain a preapproved training step or show when observations are inadequate.

## Proposed boundary

- Begin with an inert training manikin and one defined task approved by a qualified medical training partner.
- Keep gloves **advisory** in v0.1: they do not move or restrain fingers, certify clinical competence, locate unseen anatomy or authorize invasive care.
- Pause misleading cues during tracking loss, device mismatch, stale messages, radio interruption or sensor failure. Preserve free hand movement and immediate wearer cancellation.
- Keep simulated training results, clinician communication and real-world medical authority distinct. A later powered glove is a separate safety case.

## Evidence and limits

The archived proposal contains task boundaries, possible sensor roles and planned fault challenges, not a built glove or measured benefit. No accuracy, strength, latency, clinical performance, universal fit or safety qualification is established. There is no patient treatment instruction in this public note.

## Next check

Select one training task and participant population with a medical training lead. Compare conventional instruction, voice/video coaching and added tactile cues on a manikin. Independently record task errors, misleading cues, operator overrides and failures to recognize degraded sensing.
