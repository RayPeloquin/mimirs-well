# FIREGROUND FUSION HUD

**Status:** v1.0 scoped helmet-display concept; no tested firefighting equipment.

## Purpose
Propose a single, legible firefighter view combining thermal imaging with a separate edge/contrast cue when the environment permits useful sensing. The intention is to reduce mode-switching and confusion, **not** to see through every smoke or darkness condition.

## Proposed safety constraints
Keep the existing certified thermal view available without obstruction. Synchronization and uncertainty must be visible; untrustworthy edges should disappear or be marked uncertain, never presented as a confirmed door, route or safe structure. A stable image and passive failure mode matter more than an impressive overlay.

## Evidence limits
No built or validated helmet HUD, measured latency, navigation improvement, smoke-penetration capability, user fatigue result or certification for emergency work. Archived “MVP-ready” language is a proposal status, not proof of readiness.

## Next check
Start with recorded benign scenes and a thermal-only baseline; quantify registration errors, false edges and visibility limits before supervised training simulations. Operational fireground use would require qualified human-factors and safety evaluation.
