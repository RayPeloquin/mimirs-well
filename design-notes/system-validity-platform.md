# System Validity Platform

**Status:** Archived research concept; not independently validated or certified.

## Purpose and proposed mechanism
A family of state-validity checks for robotics, mobile autonomy, wearables, energy systems and industrial monitoring, meant to gate actions on measurement quality.


This concept concerns measurement validity before action, rather than the full layered decision architecture. See also [related archive note](decision-integrity-stack.md).

Example to test: a stale measurement appears plausible but no longer represents current conditions. This is a proposed failure case, not an observed result.

## Evidence limits
This is a concise public interpretation of a privately held desktop design note, not its complete technical specification. Claims of readiness, resilience, safety, accuracy or performance in the source have not been independently verified. No field trial, formal proof or regulatory approval is asserted here.

## Proposed next verification check
Build domain-specific validity thresholds and quantify stale, conflicting and spoofed-signal errors.
