# Stone Judge System

**Status:** Archived research concept; not independently validated or certified.

## Purpose and proposed mechanism
A time-filtered, non-authoritative signal display with proposed one-way isolation and dual-channel human consent. Humans assign meaning and decide whether to act.


This concept concerns a non-authoritative signal display, rather than general permission to interrupt. See also [related archive note](consent-gated-interpretation.md).

Example to test: a brief important signal disappears under temporal filtering. This is a proposed failure case, not an observed result.
