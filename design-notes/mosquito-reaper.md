# Mosquito Reaper — Non-chemical pest research

**Status:** v0.1 civilian outdoor-robotics concept; no field trial or validated suppression.

## Purpose
Explore whether mapping mosquito activity and using **contained insect capture** could reduce nuisance mosquito pressure on a defined property without open-air weapons. The source proposes aerial observation and localized capture under human supervision, but this is only an architecture sketch.

## Environmental boundaries
Do not treat every flying insect as a mosquito. The research must account for beneficial insects, bats, birds, pets, nearby people, noise, weather, local wildlife rules and human control. No autonomous pursuit of people or animals; any active element must be appropriately enclosed and independently evaluated.

## Evidence limits
No measured capture, verified species identification, non-target impact, drone integration, pest-density reduction or outdoor safety case. The source's device alternatives and team layouts are not demonstrated mechanisms or operating instructions.

## Next check
First run passive observation and species-uncertainty measurements with expert entomology review. Compare any proposed contained trap to ordinary certified traps, including non-target harm and noise before considering mobile equipment.
