# Radio Echo Chamber

**Status:** v0.1 reviewed bench concept; unbuilt and untested.

## Purpose
Explore the short-lived electromagnetic **ringdown** of a passive conductive cavity after an external radio excitation is removed. The experiment would measure a decay curve and quality factor while checking that the observed signal actually came from the cavity.

## Scope
This is a conventional resonator investigation, not a device that stores old broadcasts indefinitely. The archived inspiration was an unverified online mine video; the provenance of its audio and any alleged long-term radio retention were **not established**.

## Evidence limits
No built chamber, observed decay, independently verified quality factor or anomalous information-storage result. Recording equipment retaining a waveform is not the same thing as the chamber preserving a message.

## Next check
Define a controlled passive-resonator fixture, characterize cable and instrument leakage, repeat with detuned/empty controls, and report decay with uncertainty against textbook expectations.
