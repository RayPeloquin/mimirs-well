# Small Farm Community

**Status:** Preliminary community and operations feasibility review; no established site or licensed care service.

## Purpose
Explore a small, resident-led farm community for roughly five or six older adults, with private spaces, optional shared activities, gardens and clearly defined daily support. It aims for dignity, practical companionship and some local food production without promising complete independence from outside services.

## Decisions still open
The number of dwellings, ownership model, accessibility requirements, land and water capacity, winter operations, support staffing, emergency transport, clinical qualifications and true annual costs all require site-specific review. A shared kitchen and gardens do not themselves create a lawful care operation.

## Evidence limits
No property approval, construction budget, care license, staffing commitment, financing, full food self-sufficiency or validated operating plan. Archived review estimates are planning questions rather than established costs.

## Next check
Choose a generic feasibility template before naming a property: site access, building code and zoning, accessible circulation, utilities, food/water budgets, resident rights and a qualified-care partnership model. Compare a conservative annual operating budget to reliable funding.
