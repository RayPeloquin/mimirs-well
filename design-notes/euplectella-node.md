# Euplectella Node A / Node B

**Status:** Archived concept image only; no demonstrated prototype.

## Public concept

A biomimetic compute-pod enclosure study using a glass-sponge-inspired porous lattice to explore airflow and serviceable modular hardware.

Node-A artwork depicts a possible 3D-printed airflow lattice, ducts/fan trays, modular compute cartridges and sensors. Node-B is held alongside it as a related illustrated variant, not a proven successor.

## Archived visual reference

Euplectella Node-A Technical Compute Pod Poster.png; Euplectella Node-B Compute Pod Infographic.png. The original image material remains in the private archive; this text is a source-limited public digest.

## Evidence limits

No CAD manufacturing release, printed test article, thermal camera or sensor data, airflow/pressure measurements, electrical and fire certification, hot-swap safety proof or rack integration results are supplied. Biomimetic geometry does not ensure cooling improvement.
