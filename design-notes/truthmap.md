# TRUTHMAP — Contextual consistency research

**Status:** Speculative v0.1 architecture; no validated human assessment system.

## Purpose
Investigate whether volunteered narrative context, recollection timing and carefully governed physiological observations can be organized for human review. **TRUTHMAP is not a lie detector.** Stress, imperfect recall or inconsistency cannot establish deception.

## Proposed boundaries
Consent before observation; clear records of source, timing and uncertainty; separation of measurements from interpretations; and an accountable human reviewer. Physiological signals are context-sensitive and nonspecific. The archive proposes multimodal synchronization, but shared component names are not evidence that other research hypotheses have been validated.

## Evidence limits
No experimental validation, calibrated truth/lie discrimination, real-world deployment, or acceptable error rates have been demonstrated. Coercive uses, surveillance, employment screening or adjudication would introduce serious ethical and privacy risks.

## Next check
Freeze the consent, retention and refusal rules; test whether non-sensitive volunteer tasks outperform a plain interview baseline without inflated claims; assess error, bias and privacy impact independently.
