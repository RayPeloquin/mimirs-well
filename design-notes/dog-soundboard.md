# Dog Soundboard — Communication protocol

**Status:** Revised comparative-cognition study protocol; no new scored dog experiment.

## Purpose
Ask whether an already-trained dog can use familiar soundboard buttons to answer **new, testable prompts** under conditions that reduce owner cueing. The study evaluates one animal and one interface; it does not pronounce on language, personhood or an animal's moral worth.

## Proposed controls
Predeclare the dog's existing button meanings, keep test questions separate from training, record sessions, include owner-absent and neutral comparison conditions, and use blinded scoring. An animal leaving or avoiding the board is a **stop signal**, not a failed moral exam.

## Evidence limits
The saved PDF is a protocol and interpretation sheet. No recruited dog, registered trial, observed correct-response rate, independent replication or demonstrated interspecies language is reported. Ethical protections should not depend on passing a communication test.

## Next check
Confirm a veterinarian- and welfare-reviewed voluntary protocol, freeze the vocabulary and scoring rules, then preregister a feasible study with blinded controls and explicit stop criteria.
