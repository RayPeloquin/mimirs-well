# Choir on ARQEN — evidence versus vote order

**Status:** v0.1 pre-sprint working protocol, 12 November 2026; one practice case, no scored trials. Public summary prepared 19 November 2026.

## Purpose

Test whether a person makes a different release decision when shown the Synthetic Court's vote count before the reviewers' underlying reasons, versus seeing the reasons first. Fictional job packets have self-contained rules and answer keys; no live release authorization is delegated to a model vote.

## Proposed boundary

- Record the human operator's initial RELEASE/HOLD call and confidence before showing any review cards.
- Present the same kinds of vote/reason evidence in assigned order and record the final decision and what influenced it.
- Use a planned twelve-case, six-pair comparison with balanced answers, a concealed key and complete accounting for missing or disputed cases.
- Evaluate changes against the case rule, **not** against whether most models agree. Retain wrong-to-right and right-to-wrong changes, false releases and unnecessary holds.

## Evidence and limits

Practice packet P-01 was completed: the operator chose RELEASE with 100% confidence before and after viewing a unanimous Court vote and its reasons. This is a rehearsal, not evidence of a presentation-order effect. No scored results, preregistration, model-human accuracy benefit or generalizable behavioral finding exist in the archived protocol. The operator knows the hypothesis; with one participant and a small case set, any eventual result is local to that task.

## Next check

Freeze the case keys, balanced order assignment, outcome measures and reviewer evidence cards before running blind scored cases. Publish every completed and excluded trial and disclose preparation materials.
