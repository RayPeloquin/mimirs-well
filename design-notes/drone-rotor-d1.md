# Drone Rotor D1 — Cargo rotor geometry study

**Status:** October 2026 source review and arithmetic reconstruction; no new D1 rotor test.

## Purpose
Explore blade geometry and propulsion-system tradeoffs for civilian cargo multirotors. The archived study prioritizes fair comparisons of twist, chord, blade area, powertrain and hover/cruise operation instead of treating an unusual-looking rotor as inherently superior.

## What exists
The source documents a review of primary research and a **reconstruction of published static-propeller data for 31 samples**, plus a limited mass-sensitivity calculation and Court critique. These calculations concern existing data and assumptions; they are **not** wind-tunnel measurements, flight results or a simulation of a finished D1 geometry.

## Evidence limits
No manufactured D1 blade, measured efficiency gain, verified structural fatigue, full aircraft design, patent novelty or challenge compliance. Prior art includes passive twist, variable pitch and unconventional tips. Competition descriptions are historical context, not proof of scoring.

## Next check
Freeze one commercially measured baseline and a safe test envelope. Compare candidate variables one at a time in a validated simulation, then only under qualified bench procedures; report matched thrust, efficiency, acoustic output and uncertainty.
