# LunaRecycle Backplate

**Status:** Revision 1.0 mechanical/thermal design and calculations; no hardware cycle.

## Purpose
Explore a compact recycling press that turns clean, retired polymer film into replaceable sacrificial bench inserts for a repair workshop. A machinable insert protects the workbench below a drilled sheet-metal part; a worn insert could be replaced without discarding the fixture.

## Design boundaries
The archived plan describes an **Earth-ambient prototype first**, with operator loading, controlled pressure and heating, cooling in the tool, and inspection of the resulting tile. Mass and energy budgets are calculations, not metered outputs. Polymer source and contamination must be documented before any material-quality claim.

## Evidence limits
No pressed tile, tested mechanical performance, verified energy budget, lunar environmental qualification, flight certification or official competition score. The referenced NASA challenge had concluded; this independent design was **not an official scored entry**.

## Next check
Build a guarded bench feasibility test through qualified workshop practice; measure actual input mass, energy, tile geometry, drilling tolerance and cycle-to-cycle defects against a purchased insert.
