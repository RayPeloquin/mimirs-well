# Action Commitment Layer

**Status:** Archived research concept; not independently validated or certified.

## Purpose and proposed mechanism
A bounded commitment window that remains valid only while environment, health and authority remain within the evaluated envelope. Fallbacks require renewed safety and physical-feasibility checks.


This concept concerns continued validity after a commitment is evaluated, rather than initial permission. See also [related archive note](conduct-architecture.md).

Example to test: an approved action becomes inappropriate when the operating environment changes. This is a proposed failure case, not an observed result.

## Evidence limits
This is a concise public interpretation of a privately held desktop design note, not its complete technical specification. Claims of readiness, resilience, safety, accuracy or performance in the source have not been independently verified. No field trial, formal proof or regulatory approval is asserted here.

## Proposed next verification check
