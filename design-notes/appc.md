# Adaptive Pulse Process Control (APPC)

**Status:** v2.0 process-control concept; no measured industrial savings.

## Purpose
Explore alternating controlled work pulses and recovery periods in processes such as material handling, recycling or filtration. The hypothesis is that a dynamically adjusted duty cycle can sometimes preserve output while reducing wear or wasted energy.

## Proposed evaluation
Document load, temperature, flow, product quality and downtime, then compare a pulsed controller against a continuous baseline at **equal useful output**. Include the energy spent during each pulse, rest, restart and monitoring interval.

## Evidence limits
No measured energy reduction, improved throughput, validated yield or equipment lifetime result. Benefits will depend on the specific process and may be negative if restart losses dominate.

## Next check
Select one benign laboratory process, predeclare efficiency and quality metrics, implement conservative stop rules, and publish all trials including those with no benefit.
