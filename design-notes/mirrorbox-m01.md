# MIRRORBOX M01

**Status:** Archived concept image only; no demonstrated prototype.

## Public concept

A proposed evaluation-aware recorder that distinguishes a system's observed world state from its declared evaluation context while preserving audit traces.

The diagram separates environmental inputs from evaluation context and depicts timestamped sensor traces, requested versus observed actuator actions, an independent safety layer, and staged offline replay before any hardware-in-the-loop exercise.
