# SAFE CHANNEL — Negative Space Mapper

**Status:** Early research concept; no validated survey prototype or cleared route.

## Purpose
Explore how historical bathymetric context, repeat surveys and independent observations might flag discrepancies in a maritime mapping record for expert review. The aim is **survey assurance**, not automatic clearance of waters or navigation certification.

## Conceptual approach
Record a baseline and survey provenance; compare successive observations with environmental context; preserve uncertainty and request independent confirmation when reports disagree. An operator evaluates candidate anomalies and limitations. The public description intentionally excludes tactical operating parameters.

## Evidence limits
No field trial, measured sensitivity, false-alarm rate, detection probability, certified navigation safety, or operational decision capability has been demonstrated. A lack of mapped anomalies is not evidence that a route is safe.

## Next check
Define a non-operational synthetic survey dataset, known uncertainty labels and an independent review rubric; measure false positives and missed discrepancies before considering any supervised real-world study.
