# Ghost Fiber — Neutrino evidence replay

**Status:** v1.3 revised offline software specification; no benchmark or new detector.

## Purpose
Explore a fault-tolerant **evidence and replay layer** for candidate neutrino observations from independently operated instruments. The goal is reproducible handling of clock drift, calibration changes, missing messages and correction records—not new neutrino physics.

## Revision boundary
The archived v1.3 review withdrew earlier claims of already-established technical novelty or a proposed new worldwide detector deployment. It acknowledges existing federated early-warning and analysis work, including SNEWS, as prior art. The word *fiber* refers to a digital evidence chain, not neutrino storage.

## Evidence limits
No implemented GHOST FIBER benchmark, deployed detector integration, sensitivity improvement, confirmed supernova event, verified novelty or anomalous signal. Proposed statistical residuals require explicit null models and uncertainty controls.

## Next check
Freeze an offline message/replay format and fault-injection cases. Compare candidate reconstruction and alert provenance against an established baseline without delaying the ordinary scientific candidate path.
