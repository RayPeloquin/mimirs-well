# Sound Bus — Shipboard sensing and power integration

**Status:** Concept summary only; no validated naval platform or field trial.

## Purpose
Explore broad engineering interfaces between shipboard electrical distribution, maintenance diagnostics and distributed acoustic observation. A useful architecture would document where sensing, power quality, equipment self-noise, audit logs and operator authorization interact.

## Lineage note
The catalog title **Sound Bus** is attached to an archived source headed **HIPPO BUS v2.1 (NAVAL / SUBHUNTER — CLEAN)**. It may be related to the existing HIPPO Bus record; the design family, alias and independent scope are **not verified**.

## Public boundaries
This digest covers only general systems engineering, separation of sensing from control, and operator-governed active measurements. It intentionally omits tactical acoustic methods, frequencies, evasion/engagement scenarios and sensitive operating parameters.

## Evidence limits
No measured acoustic performance, installed integration, field trial, platform qualification or independent validation. Naming overlap is not proof of a tested subsystem.
