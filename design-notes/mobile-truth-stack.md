# Mobile Truth Stack

**Status:** Archived research concept; not independently validated or certified.

## Purpose and proposed mechanism
Portable decision support that fuses existing sensor and operator signals, tags uncertainty and presents restrained confidence-aware overlays.


This concept concerns presentation of uncertainty to an operator, rather than general measurement gating. See also [related archive note](system-validity-platform.md).

Example to test: an overlay retains an outdated confidence indication after a sensor drops out. This is a proposed failure case, not an observed result.

## Evidence limits
This is a concise public interpretation of a privately held desktop design note, not its complete technical specification. Claims of readiness, resilience, safety, accuracy or performance in the source have not been independently verified. No field trial, formal proof or regulatory approval is asserted here.

## Proposed next verification check
Replay a public field dataset, measure confidence calibration, signal loss and operator utility.
