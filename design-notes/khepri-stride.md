# Khepri Stride — Sneaker concept

**Status:** Archived concept image only; no demonstrated prototype.

## Public concept

A speculative performance-shoe design combining an energy-return sole, low-power accent lighting and a proposed body-motion/heat harvesting layer.

The poster depicts a sneaker with stylized energy-return construction and edge lights; its captions suggest harvesting mechanical or thermal energy during ordinary wear.
