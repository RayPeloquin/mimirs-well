# Diamond Thermoelectric Power Source

**Status:** v0.1 calculation-led space power concept; no physical prototype or measured diamond advantage.

## Purpose
Test whether a synthetic diamond heat-spreader interface could make a compact thermoelectric power system more repeatable under a declared thermal load. The archived study frames a long-duration instrument power budget and a separate, simpler **materials-comparison experiment**.

## Important distinction
Diamond may redistribute heat, but it does not create energy. Overall electrical output depends on the complete thermal-to-electric conversion and heat rejection, including losses. Any hypothetical advantage over copper or a direct-contact baseline must survive remounting and independent measurement.

## Evidence limits
No measured conversion efficiency, demonstrated heat-spreader gain, flight-qualified radiator, thermal-source qualification or twenty-year operating result. The archived source's power and longevity figures are requirements and assumptions, not achieved performance. Potential specialized heat sources require independent expert governance; this public note contains no sourcing or assembly instructions.

## Next check
Use a benign electrically heated bench comparison with matched geometry, contact force and uncertainty; measure heat flow and electrical output before proposing any space-qualified integration.
