# Wi-Fi Sensing for Accessible Homes

**Status:** Feasibility review only; no detector built or evaluated.

## Purpose
Explore whether ordinary Wi-Fi signal variations in approved recordings can distinguish movement from quiet periods for a voluntary accessibility aid. The initial objective is a **replay-only research test**, not in-home surveillance or emergency detection.

## Archived basis
The November 2026 Court review compared publicly described datasets and recommended a small labeled subset, a transparent baseline and evaluation on untouched recording sessions. Dataset listings and one README were inspected; the underlying signal recordings were **not downloaded or analyzed**. A stationary occupant and an empty room may look alike, so movement and occupancy are separate tasks.

## Evidence limits
No measured sensitivity, missed-event rate, false alarm rate, in-home installation, or clinical benefit. Neither dataset descriptions nor agreement among reviewers establishes detection performance or safety.

## Next check
Review label definitions and consent conditions; hold out complete recording sessions; report balanced accuracy, misses, false alarms and room-to-room variation against a simple baseline. Before any home pilot, establish opt-in, retention limits and explicit non-emergency status.
