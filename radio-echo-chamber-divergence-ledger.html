01 / The three questions

Keep intent, evidence and hypothesis apart.

01 / INTENDED

What we intended

Investigate conventional electromagnetic cavity ringdown after a radio source is removed, with a decay curve, quality factor and explicit instrument-leakage controls.

A goal is not an experimental result.

02 / DOCUMENTED

What actually happened

The inspiring online mine-broadcast account was unverified. No built chamber, measured ringdown, verified quality factor or historic-broadcast retention is reported. A recording instrument replaying audio would not show cavity storage.

This describes the available source record; no new tests are implied.

03 / POSSIBLE CROSSOVER

What else might this be useful for?

The ordinary, defensible crossover is a low-risk RF metrology and education rig for fitting losses and separating leakage from passive resonance. The proposed fixture itself remains untested.

New hypothesis only; independent validation needed.

Evidence boundary: Conventional short-lived resonance does not demonstrate stored historical broadcasts or anomalous information persistence.
02 / Next reproducible brick

What would change the record?
