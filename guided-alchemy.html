Can two independent checks catch false signals?

Trust a measurement | Ghostglass + Chihuahua Sphere / CS-1

Why investigate?

Ghostglass tests whether counts differ; Chihuahua Sphere tracks energy crossing boundaries. A separate optical ledger may flag some misleading detections.

Proposed model (not derived truth)

Keep the statistical result and independently measured optical energy residual separate. Compare statistics alone, statistics plus a residual review flag, and conventional quality control at matched sensitivity. A discrepancy flags review, not automatic rejection.

Proposed falsification test

In blinded synthetic trials with known nulls and injected signals, compare false alarms at matched sensitivity across the three arms. Record inconclusive outcomes.

Limits and refusal conditions

Counts are not joules. Independent calibration and uncertainty bounds are required; a ledger constructed from the same detector counts is circular. No optical apparatus or joint experiment has been validated.

When does old information become misleading?

Trust a measurement | SPOTRECON / SPOTRECON R2 + CONFLUENCE-01 — Synthetic Sense

Why investigate?

One source measures observation age; the other proposes false events per valid hour. Their combination asks how age affects reliability.

Proposed model (not derived truth)

For a synthetic timestamped data stream, report false-event rate separately for predefined observation-age bins. Retain unavailable duration and coverage as separate outputs; compare with an age-blind baseline.

Proposed falsification test

Inject known synthetic delays, dropouts and ground-truth events. Compare false-event rates, coverage and missed events for a frozen freshness policy versus the baseline.

Limits and refusal conditions

Age is not error or false-event rate. Clock synchronization, event definitions and valid-duration accounting matter; excluding stale data may hide failures. No field or sensory performance demonstrated.

Where does the energy really go?

Account for resources | Chihuahua Sphere / CS-1 + Adventurer’s Forge

Why investigate?

The optical ledger tracks delivered energy; the Carnot ceiling bounds an ideal heat engine. Separate them to avoid claiming all energy losses can become useful work.

Proposed model (not derived truth)

For a hypothetical separate heat-engine stage, keep optical/electrical conversion and heat-engine efficiency in separate ledgers. Bound the latter by its stated absolute temperatures. Do not add intermediate transfers to total output or equate the two efficiencies.

Proposed falsification test

Construct a synthetic energy budget with explicit boundaries and compare it with a deliberately double-counted budget. Check conservation and the ideal thermal ceiling independently.

Limits and refusal conditions

Carnot applies only to a reversible two-reservoir heat engine, not direct photovoltaic conversion. No recoverable heat quantity or actual conversion performance is supplied.

Did the material arrive, or merely move?

Account for resources | Zion Twin-Ring Tuna Habitat + SpinCell DLE

Why investigate?

Both sources report a fraction of a conserved quantity, but the dye fraction and lithium product recovery describe different systems.

Proposed model (not derived truth)

Define separate normalized accounting metrics for synthetic tracer transfer and material recovery. Compare their reported fractions only with explicit initial/input boundaries, product or receiving compartment, residual inventory and uncertainty.

Proposed falsification test

Use synthetic mass-balance tables with known missing outlets and inventory changes. Compare complete and incomplete accounts; verify that closure errors are identified.

Limits and refusal conditions

Dye concentration sums and lithium mass are not interchangeable. Equal-area grid assumptions apply to Zion; its mesh-dependent results do not establish physical transfer. SpinCell has no measured extraction data.

Is a system failing often or being observed longer?

Test a system | CRUCIBLE + CONFLUENCE-01 — Synthetic Sense

Why investigate?

A fraction of failed runs and a rate per hour answer different questions. Reporting both may expose misleading comparisons.

Proposed model (not derived truth)

For an inanimate synthetic benchmark, record violation fraction per conclusive exercised run and false-event rate per valid observation hour separately. Stratify by scenario duration and report coverage.

Proposed falsification test

Compare synthetic groups with equal failure fractions but unequal valid durations, then with equal rates but unequal run lengths. Verify the metrics are not conflated.

Limits and refusal conditions

A contract violation is not necessarily a false event. Runs and hours have different denominators. No real-world reliability or safety benefit follows.

Can a numerical artifact masquerade as a finding?

Test a system | SKEPTIC-1 + Temporal Acoustics

Why investigate?

One source computes natural modes; the other records a numerical time-step stability guard. Both illustrate the importance of checking a model before interpreting outputs.

Proposed model (not derived truth)

Keep the two source models independent. In a toy numerical comparison, record modal outputs and convergence diagnostics separately from scalar-wave time-step acceptance. Treat a numerical guard as a prerequisite for interpretation, not evidence of a physical mode.

Proposed falsification test

Use the existing ideal models independently, compare results at refined numerical settings where applicable, and record failed convergence or stability checks instead of interpreting them as physical effects.

Limits and refusal conditions

A lumped stiffness-mass model and a scalar-wave discretization are not the same physical system. The wave stability bound does not establish the accuracy of either model or a coupling between them.
