01 / The three questions

Keep intent, evidence and hypothesis apart.

01 / INTENDED

What we intended

Assess whether known neutrino interactions can produce a statistically recognizable nuclear-conversion event against a specified background.

A goal is not an experimental result.

02 / DOCUMENTED

What actually happened

The public digest rejects the leap from favorable stochastic interaction-rate calculations to control of one chosen atom. It reports no apparatus, working detector, practical yield or external Court approval. The reported review is calculations only.

This describes the available source record; no new tests are implied.

03 / POSSIBLE CROSSOVER

What else might this be useful for?

The credible alternate question concerns rare-event detection and prompt/delayed evidence under explicit background and efficiency uncertainty, not directed atom editing.

New hypothesis only; independent validation needed.

Evidence boundary: A tagged reaction does not demonstrate atomic editing or a working neutrino device.
02 / Next reproducible brick

What would change the record?

1 · Preserve

Request specialist review of reaction and primary nuclear data.

2 · Measure

Reproduce flux, cross-section and background sensitivity against published data.

3 · Challenge

Treat any proposed observation as stochastic and avoid claims of atomic addressability.
