Does the sender know the message is still queued?

The fallback diagram leaves delivery confirmation under loss of coverage untested.

Illustrated concept

What we intended

The Sky variant combines a foldable paper-based interface with a removable communications module. The drawing assigns calls and texts to cellular service and short emergency messages to satellite fallback.

What the source documents

The artwork supplies no coverage trials, battery endurance measurements or confirmed delivery records.

Possible crossover

Simulated outages could test status wording for other devices that send messages intermittently.

Next test

Simulate loss of coverage and delayed acknowledgements. Check whether users can distinguish a queued message from confirmed delivery.
