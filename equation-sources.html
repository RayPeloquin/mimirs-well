skeptic-1

Ideal numerical mass–spring model; no measured hardware data

lambda_i = eigenvalue_i(K, M)
f_i = sqrt(lambda_i) / (2*pi)

Natural frequencies are calculated from the generalized stiffness–mass eigenvalues of an ideal three-mass model.

lambda_i
i-th generalized eigenvalue (1/s^2)
K
stiffness matrix (N/m)
M
mass matrix (kg)
f_i
i-th natural frequency (Hz)

Faithful notation for scipy.linalg.eigh(K,M) and sqrt(vals)/(2*pi), not a separately printed equation in the original Python. No damping, friction, or sensor physics.

echo-rx

Reduced theoretical mass-balance equation; no biological results

dA_T/dt = k_out*A_D - (k_clear + k_spill)*A_T

The target compartment receives active mass from a depot and loses it through clearance and spillover.

A_T
active protein mass in target pool
t
time
k_out
depot-to-target transfer rate (1/time)
A_D
active protein mass in depot pool
k_clear
target clearance rate (1/time)
k_spill
target spillover rate (1/time)

Assumes homogeneous linear compartments and fixed rates; does not establish effective biological shutdown, therapeutic exposure or clinical safety.

ghostglass

Synthetic on/off statistical test; no physical detector validation

p = BinomSF(n_on - 1; n_on + n_off, 1/(1+r))

Computes a conditional on/off Poisson-background tail probability using the binomial survival function.

p
one-sided exact conditional p-value
n_on
nonnegative on-window event count
n_off
nonnegative off-window event count
r
off/on exposure ratio (>0)

BinomSF(k;n,q)=P(X>k) for X~Binomial(n,q). Assumes stationary equal on/off background rates, valid exposures and Poisson counts; not a signal discovery claim.

Ghostglass experiment.py · decide() exact conditional test

Synthetic 2D scalar-wave numerical method; no field measurements

c*dt/dx < 1/sqrt(2)

The scalar finite-difference pilot rejects time steps that violate its two-dimensional wave propagation stability bound.

dt
simulation time step (time)
dx
simulation grid spacing (length)

Transcribed from the code’s strict inequality and separate velocity-field check. A numerical stability constraint, not an acoustic discovery or elastic-earth constitutive law.

eta_receiver = E_load / E_incident

The receiver and whole apparatus have different efficiency denominators. Incident light is an internal transfer, not another output.

Notation transcribed from the public prose and hypothetical 10 J / 2 J example. Positive denominators and matched intervals required. No measured efficiency or novel energy gain.

the-traveler-solar-electric-research-airship

Historical passive-drift simulation; no flight or recovery test

lap = (abs(lon - lon_start) >= 360 deg)
approach = lap_already_observed AND (d <= r)

The historical benchmark counts net longitude laps and only counts sampled approaches once a lap has been observed.

lap
Boolean lap threshold at a sample
lon
unwrapped sampled longitude (degrees)
lon_start
starting longitude (degrees)
approach
Boolean sampled post-lap approach event
lap_already_observed
lap observed at or before current sample
d
great-circle distance to start (km)
r
benchmark approach radius (km)

Scoring rule, not trajectory planning, steering or flight control. Net longitude is not cumulative absolute path length. Fixed-pressure passive replay; no descent, landing, physical recovery or airspace authorization established.

zion-twin-ring-tuna-habitat

Dimensionless dye-transport simulation; no physical tank trial

F_dye_percent = 100 * C_receiving / C_initial

The reported dye metric is the sum of dye concentration in receiving-loop cells divided by the initial total dye sum, expressed as a percentage.

F_dye_percent
receiving-loop dye percentage
C_receiving
sum of dye concentrations in receiving cells at sample time
C_initial
initial sum of dye concentrations over all wet cells

Equal-area cells within a run; initial dye sum positive. This metric is distinct from outer-straight circulation and one-way gate flux. Strong mesh dependence, no established physical exchange rate, gate-motion result or animal welfare outcome.

test_gate.py, simulate() return value; bundled README

adventurer-s-forge

Desk-checked tabletop scenario using conventional thermodynamics; no hardware validation

eta_Carnot = 1 - T_cold / T_hot

The reversible heat-engine efficiency ceiling depends on the absolute sink and source temperatures.

eta_Carnot
maximum reversible thermal efficiency fraction
T_cold
absolute cold-reservoir temperature (K)
T_hot
absolute hot-reservoir temperature (K)

Positive absolute temperatures with T_hot > T_cold; ideal reversible two-reservoir bound. Does not predict real efficiency or establish a fictional energy source, cooling system or working hardware.
