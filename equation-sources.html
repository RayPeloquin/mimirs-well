skeptic-1

Ideal numerical mass–spring model; no measured hardware data

lambda_i = eigenvalue_i(K, M)
f_i = sqrt(lambda_i) / (2*pi)

Natural frequencies are calculated from the generalized stiffness–mass eigenvalues of an ideal three-mass model.

lambda_i
i-th generalized eigenvalue (1/s^2)
K
stiffness matrix (N/m)
M
mass matrix (kg)
f_i
i-th natural frequency (Hz)

Faithful notation for scipy.linalg.eigh(K,M) and sqrt(vals)/(2*pi), not a separately printed equation in the original Python. No damping, friction, or sensor physics.

echo-rx

Reduced theoretical mass-balance equation; no biological results

dA_T/dt = k_out*A_D - (k_clear + k_spill)*A_T

The target compartment receives active mass from a depot and loses it through clearance and spillover.

A_T
active protein mass in target pool
t
time
k_out
depot-to-target transfer rate (1/time)
A_D
active protein mass in depot pool
k_clear
target clearance rate (1/time)
k_spill
target spillover rate (1/time)

Assumes homogeneous linear compartments and fixed rates; does not establish effective biological shutdown, therapeutic exposure or clinical safety.

ghostglass

Synthetic on/off statistical test; no physical detector validation

p = BinomSF(n_on - 1; n_on + n_off, 1/(1+r))

Computes a conditional on/off Poisson-background tail probability using the binomial survival function.

p
one-sided exact conditional p-value
n_on
nonnegative on-window event count
n_off
nonnegative off-window event count
r
off/on exposure ratio (>0)

BinomSF(k;n,q)=P(X>k) for X~Binomial(n,q). Assumes stationary equal on/off background rates, valid exposures and Poisson counts; not a signal discovery claim.

temporal-acoustics

Synthetic 2D scalar-wave numerical method; no field measurements

c*dt/dx < 1/sqrt(2)

The scalar finite-difference pilot rejects time steps that violate its two-dimensional wave propagation stability bound.

c
maximum scalar-wave speed (length/time)
dt
simulation time step (time)
dx
simulation grid spacing (length)

Transcribed from the code’s strict inequality and separate velocity-field check. A numerical stability constraint, not an acoustic discovery or elastic-earth constitutive law.
