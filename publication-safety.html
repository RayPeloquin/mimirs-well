Two review stages, followed by a decision and deployment check

  1. Owner's source and purpose review: confirm the exact file/revision, provenance, intended civilian benefit, misuse potential and combined disclosure across projects.
  2. Separate qualified technical/safety review: evaluate whether the proposed release would make hazardous construction or deployment more actionable. AI Court critique alone does not constitute independent qualification.
  3. Explicit owner decision: approve a bounded safe summary, revise, defer, or withhold the specific revision. No decision means withhold.
  4. Verify deployed material: check public pages, repository history, code, images, downloads and linked sources after a release.

What remains publishable

Purpose, evidence status, known limitations, ethical boundaries, and non-operational research discussion. Without a reviewed release decision, do not publish detailed apparatus construction, high-energy implementations, operation/tuning plans, or assembled reproduction packages.

Important limitation: a public GitHub project is not private, including historic commits and downloadable copies. Website labels and automated tripwires are not access controls. Keep original restricted sources outside the public repository.
