Where did the lithium go?

An outlet can look enriched even when much of the incoming lithium leaves in another stream.

Illustrated concept

What we intended

A contactor mixes lithium-bearing brine with a capture phase and uses rotation to separate the streams. Filtration precedes the contactor.

What the source documents

The illustration gives no paired inlet and outlet assays from which to calculate lithium recovery or selectivity.

Possible crossover

The same accounting could compare separation designs that trade recovery for concentrate purity.

Next test

Account for lithium in every outlet of one batch and compare recovery and energy use with a stirred-and-settled baseline.
