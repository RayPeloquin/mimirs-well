Summary

A reported three-site scalar-field toy-model investigation of quantum information spreading during a modeled bounce, comparing harmonic and interacting cases.

Evidence and limits

Poster reports a negative result for high-fidelity redundant quantum error correction: two-site recovery improved versus the identity reference, but remained imperfect. Reported values are not independently reproduced; code, exact initial encoding, recovery optimization, numerical convergence and a preregistered threshold are not supplied. This toy model does not establish cosmological information survival.
