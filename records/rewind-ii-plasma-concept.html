REWIND-II — Plasma concept

Record ID: rewind-ii-plasma-concept | Expanded record

Concept or design review

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

Proposed solid-state air-moving concept based on ion-neutral momentum transfer and a wide, low-velocity-increment airflow.

Evidence and limits

Electroaerodynamic propulsion has experimental precedent; this proposed traveling electromagnetic architecture, high mass flow, efficiency, acoustic performance and reliability are not demonstrated. Static magnet arrays alone do not establish net axial momentum transfer. No measured data supplied.
