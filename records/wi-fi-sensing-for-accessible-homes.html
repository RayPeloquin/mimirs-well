Summary

A voluntary accessibility research concept testing whether Wi-Fi signal variation can distinguish movement from quiet intervals in preexisting consent-appropriate recordings.

Evidence and limits

No detector was built or tested, and archived reviewers inspected dataset listings rather than processing recordings. No measured false-alarm or missed-event rate, occupancy inference, home installation or emergency service.
