Summary

Proposed external visible, near-infrared and passive thermal sensor fusion to extend accessible visual information. Bench-only initial phase; separate exploratory acoustic analysis concept.

Evidence and limits

Concept and proposed tests only. No hardware tests, improved biological perception, animal safety, communication decoding or validated optical performance established. The simulator illustrates weights, not measured sensor or feline performance.
