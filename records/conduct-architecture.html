Summary

A proposed authorization boundary between planning and execution, intended to review action scope and retain explicit human oversight while leaving physical control to independent lower-level safeguards.

Evidence and limits

The reviewed artifact describes a mandatory gate, review/refusal concepts and audit requirements. It supplies no implementation, bypass testing, measured overhead or evidence that the boundary cannot be bypassed. Model agreement is not an enforcement mechanism. This website update reviewed the saved artifact; it did not rerun the underlying research.
