Adaptive Qubit Allocator

Expanded record · evidence type uncategorized

Summary

A proposed classical resource scheduler that uses reported calibration metrics and job priority to assign eligible physical qubits before execution, with a capped reserve policy and fairness constraints.

Evidence and limits

The state block is a control-layer specification, not an implemented scheduler or measured performance study. No demonstrated fidelity gain, hardware benchmark, resource availability improvement or quantum-state transfer is established.
