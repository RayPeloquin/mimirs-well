Summary

Proposed two-state quantum scattering model exploring whether coherent internal-state dynamics can alter transmission.

Evidence and limits

The displayed two-level Hamiltonian has consistent local dressed eigenvalues. This alone does not establish a switch, coherent recombination, or suppression of total transmission by decoherence; these depend on preparation, scattering dynamics and detection. The labeled detuning is not explicitly defined in the Hamiltonian. No numerical or experimental transmission result supplied.
