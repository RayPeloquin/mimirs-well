Summary

A reconfigurable low-voltage optical experiment using adjustable collection panels and separate energy balances for the lamp and receiver. Light reaching the panel is an intermediate transfer. Whole-apparatus efficiency compares electricity delivered to the load with total electricity supplied.

Evidence and limits

No apparatus, empirical energy collection efficiency, novel power source or observed benefit from a spherical shell. Geometry effects are proposed for comparison.
