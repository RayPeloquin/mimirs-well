Summary

An autonomy-testing demonstrator using uncalibrated MuJoCo rover and quadrotor simulations, mission contracts, noisy delayed sensors, actuator limits and replay controls. Controllers are manually authored; the legacy baseline is deliberately defective.

Evidence and limits

Published validation records report 37 passing checks for current source, 36 for the earlier replay-integrity patch, and 34 for the historical ZIP and explorer. These counts must not be substituted for one another. This website update reviewed the overview; it did not rerun validation. Original held-out campaign: rover contract violations 8/40 legacy versus 0/40 guarded; quadrotor 31/40 legacy versus 27/40 guarded. Quadrotor pairs include five improvements and one regression. All 160 evaluations agreed with an independent Boolean oracle; six replay checks matched and four deliberate state/source changes were detected. Zero rover violations in 40 synthetic cases does not establish zero risk; the reported 95% Wilson upper bound is 8.8%. Oracle agreement and exact replay can share modeling or specification mistakes. Current patches check trace checksums, equal replay verdicts and checkpoint timestep compatibility. Historical campaign results remain unchanged and have not been recertified. Patched source reports historical traces as incompatible when source hashes differ. No hardware validity, flight readiness, field reliability or automatic defect repair is established.
