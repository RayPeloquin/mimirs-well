HIPPO-MAG-01 — Magnetic-compensation experiment

Summary

A bounded magnetic-compensation research concept asking whether controlled coil currents can reduce a fixed source's anomaly at declared measurement locations, with independently assessed residuals.

Evidence and limits

The archive reports a runnable static planning surrogate and 28 labeled sequential software checks, not hardware results. Compensation improves the chosen validation-shell RMS in that surrogate but worsens some nearby sampled points, so uniform cancellation is not established. There is no hardware protection, hold-up, current-decay or full apparatus validation. This website update reviewed saved documentation; it did not rerun models or conduct experiments.
