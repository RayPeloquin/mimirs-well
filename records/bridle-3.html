Summary

A modular aerospike concept exploring six replaceable hot modules on a shared cooled frame, candidate temperature-actuated thermal contacts and local reserve cooling. The current screening model investigates shutdown and subsequent heat soak under assumed cooling faults.

Evidence and limits

The simulation is uncalibrated against engine tests. Temperatures are dimensionless ratios of module-to-coolant temperature rise relative to startup; model time is scaled by heat capacity divided by nominal cooling conductance. Values are not degrees or seconds without measured hardware parameters. One-module fault over 12 model time units, with 20% main conductance retained: separated modules peak at 1.852, joined at 1.453, ideal switched interfaces at 1.474, and local reserve at 1.205. Switches plus reserve also give 1.205 because the switch threshold is never reached; reserve cooling accounts for the improvement in that case. Shared complete main-cooling loss: identical modules and reserves exchange no net heat. The original reserve approaches 2.102392 against the chosen 1.50 limit. A reserve sized only through model time 12 later approaches 1.529371. The all-time exponential threshold approaches 1.500000 with zero model margin. These are model comparisons, not qualified hardware requirements. The README describes checks for reciprocal flux conservation, exact solutions, a matrix-exponential comparison, switch-step convergence and remaining heat after reserve cutoff. Its JavaScript harness checks eight reference cases, controls, warnings and CSV export. This website update reviewed the public overview and did not rerun those checks. No built-engine safety, thrust, specific impulse, equal-mass advantage or validated operating envelope is established.
