Summary

A proposed catalog architecture linking design names, versions, evidence, review status, source lineage, reusable patterns and next actions, with distinct public and private views.

Evidence and limits

The source proposes structured entries, statuses and combination views. It does not demonstrate an automated combination engine, revenue benefit, patent readiness or complete archive migration. The working Well demonstrates a browsable public catalog, not every capability in this older proposal. This website update reviewed saved documentation; it did not rerun models or conduct experiments.
