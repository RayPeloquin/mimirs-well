Summary

A therapeutic research branch of ECHO-M proposing contained cultured mammalian cells and a tissue model: sense one specified inflammatory input, produce one active protein and measure its effect and residual activity after production is disabled. The proposed first benchmark output is IL-1 receptor antagonist (IL-1Ra).

Evidence and limits

No ECHO-Rx biological or clinical results. The contained biological system, carrier implementation and molecular enable/disable mechanism remain unbuilt and unqualified. Related published engineered-cell studies provide precedents, not validation of this proposed combination. A published standard-library shutdown script compares an analytical reduced two-compartment solution with numerical RK4 integration. The README reports checks of the equal-rate limit, mass balance, integrated tail and a steady-state case. This website update did not rerun the script. With arbitrary normalized inputs, its loaded-depot example has a post-stop target peak of 1.2804815896 at time 1.7027520792 and integrated tail 11. These are mathematical illustrations, not measured biological performance, doses or design targets. The example assumes an ideal instantaneous stop of further secretion, fixed coefficients, no leak and no new death-release input; it does not validate the full biological model. Production OFF, residual protein clearance, cell removal, chromosome persistence and tissue recovery are separate endpoints. A reporter decrease does not by itself establish safe shutdown or recovery. HAC non-integration remains a requirement to investigate, not an established property.
