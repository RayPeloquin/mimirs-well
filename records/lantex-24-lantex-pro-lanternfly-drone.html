Summary

Recovered LANTEX-24/LANTEX-PRO lanternfly-drone titles, reviewed alongside the related public Lanternfly Collector concept: locate and confirm targets, collect them into a sealed cartridge, and return to a separate processing dock.

Evidence and limits

The public Collector overview describes an early untested concept and proposed ground tests followed by flight tests. No capture effectiveness, unintended-catch rate, clogging result or boom flight-dynamics measurement is reported. The archived LANTEX labels alone do not establish an implemented variant.

Contributors

Lanternfly Collector is presented in Ray Peloquin's public repository. Detailed contributions and lineage to the LANTEX variants remain to be documented.

Source revision
