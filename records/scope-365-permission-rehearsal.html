Summary

An offline Microsoft 365 permission-rehearsal prototype: show modeled grant routes, remove selected edges in a copy, and search again for surviving unwanted routes and required access lost by the proposed cleanup.

Evidence and limits

The brief reports 22 software tests using synthetic data, including alternate routes, broad-cleanup damage, expiry, stale evidence, malformed inputs and report escaping. This website update did not rerun them. A modeled route establishes graph reachability only. A missing route cannot certify denied live access, and live controls may block a modeled grant. No live tenant, connector, enforcement change, compliance result or Copilot-readiness claim is established.
