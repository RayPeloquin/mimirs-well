ECHO-M

Record ID: echo-m | Expanded record

Concept or design review

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

A proposed host-coupled artificial chromosome recorder in cultured mammalian cells: a live reporter tracks one defined host-state input, a separate one-bit DNA latch could retain exposure evidence, and an external ledger links readouts to their observation unit and window.

Evidence and limits

No ECHO-M construct or experimental results are reported. Component precedents do not validate the integrated system. Retention, shutoff and confidence calculations in the brief are illustrative, not measured performance. Proposed interpretations are recorded, qualified blank and unknown. A one-bit mark gives neither an exact time nor an event count. A qualified blank still permits missed exposures at the tested sensitivity. Unresolved timing, missing readouts, silencing, loss and death must remain visible in the enrolled denominator. The intended separation from host chromosomes and avoidance of host-genome editing require physical-location and unintended-integration measurements with declared detection limits. They are not established properties of a built ECHO-M construct. The public source distinguishes artificial-chromosome formation in cited human-cell work from long-term stability, which that follow-up left untested. No clinical readiness or established novelty is claimed.
