PULSELINK — Reentry

Record ID: pulselink-reentry | Expanded record

Concept or design review

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

Proposed intermittent communication protocol for civilian atmospheric reentry, using temporary improvements in plasma-channel transparency to exchange telemetry.

Evidence and limits

The referenced Rodriguez Fuentes and Parent Physics of Fluids study (2026; arXiv:2512.18163) reports numerical plasma depletion and lower RF attenuation. It does not validate the illustrated communications protocol, post-pulse window duration, link reliability or vehicle-scale power budget. No prototype or flight data supplied.
