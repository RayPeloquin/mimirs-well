Artificial Heart — Wireless power and dual reserves

Summary

A system-level artificial-heart research proposal investigating wireless power and isolated energy reserves alongside established pumping principles.

Evidence and limits

The archive reports an executed two-bank energy model with ten scenarios checked against closed forms and conservation. This is numerical consistency, not device validation. Simulated bank loss is already isolated/open circuit; it does not demonstrate short isolation, thermal containment, voltage stability, circulation or physiological outcomes. No device has been built or tested. This website update reviewed the saved source; it did not rerun models or conduct experiments.

Contributors

Prepared for Ray Peloquin. Existing pumping, wireless-power and dual-battery approaches are explicitly credited as prior art; no new pumping principle or product superiority is claimed.
