Summary

A proposed status-only resource-reporting layer for the modular water stack, intended to communicate availability or maintenance needs without commanding equipment or allocating aid.

Evidence and limits

The reviewed source defines reporting boundaries, not a deployed network or measured delivery, resilience or security performance. A status message does not establish equipment state, treatment verification or resource entitlement. This website update reviewed the saved source; it did not rerun models or conduct experiments.
