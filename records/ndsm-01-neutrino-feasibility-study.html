NDSM-01 — Neutrino feasibility study

Record ID: ndsm-01-neutrino-feasibility-study | Expanded record

Calculation, prediction, or simulation

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

Assess whether known neutrino interactions permit statistically identified nuclear-conversion events, separately from the unsupported goal of selecting and changing one chosen atom on demand.

Evidence and limits

The source works through illustrative interaction-rate arguments and identifies weak-interaction precedent. It provides no built device, selected-atom control, new interaction, working detector, practical conversion yield or external Court approval.
