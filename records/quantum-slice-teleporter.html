Summary

A speculative scan-and-rebuild study in which a digital description could guide reconstruction from local material. Distinguish this classical information-processing question from quantum state teleportation.

Evidence and limits

The pictured chamber does not establish object transfer. Quantum teleportation transfers quantum-state information through shared entanglement and classical communication; it is not matter transport. Classical reconstruction depends on available measurements, fabrication capability, and local material. Proposed test only: use simple synthetic voxel objects with frozen incomplete scan masks and compare naive filling against a constrained reconstruction algorithm at equal input information. Report voxel error, ambiguity, and confidence; reject a perfect-reconstruction claim if two different objects produce the same scan or if the candidate fails to improve the baseline on held-out cases. No quantum experiment, physical fabrication, object transfer or human application is demonstrated.
