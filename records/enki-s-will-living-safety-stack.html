Summary

A proposed learning-and-memory safety architecture in which adaptive recommendations remain separate from authority to command. In the Triad concept it supplies learning without independently expanding control permissions. Recovered artwork: An abstract visualization of the proposed safety, memory and learning architecture.

Evidence and limits

The reviewed materials establish a design doctrine and intended role. They do not provide a measured learning system, deployed enforcement evidence or an experimental safety result. Internal doctrine-locked labels do not certify implementation. Artwork review: Concept artwork only. Many embedded labels are unreadable or malformed; they are not accepted as component definitions, specifications or evidence of an implemented nervous system.
