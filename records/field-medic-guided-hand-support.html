FIELD MEDIC — Guided-hand support

Record ID: field-medic-guided-hand-support | Expanded record

Expanded record · evidence type uncategorized

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

A nonclinical wearable guided-hand concept using backpack/hip support, articulated arm supports and gloves around the wearer's own hands. Intended assistance remains task-bounded and human-controlled.

Evidence and limits

The reviewed six-page brief supplies proposed requirements and illustrative arithmetic, not new device measurements. Current development boundary is inert fixtures and mannequins. Passive support, cues/rehearsal, bounded powered guidance and fault response have distinct requirements. Logical revocation does not prove zero residual motion or force. No clinical capability, broad novice suitability or autonomous trauma-care benefit is established. This website update reviewed the saved source; it did not rerun the research or perform hardware tests.

Contributors
