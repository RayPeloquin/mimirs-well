Summary

A proposed diagnostic-only layer for alerting a human when a governed system may be refusing, throttling or isolating more than justified.

Evidence and limits

The artifact supplies candidate indicators and an alert concept, not calibrated rigidity detection, verified read-only isolation or measured operator benefit. Frequent refusal alone does not show that a refusal is wrong. Monitoring-failure visibility and the burden of extra alerts remain to be tested. This website pass reviewed saved documentation; it did not rerun the underlying system or conduct physical/clinical experiments.
