Blood Manager

Record ID: blood-manager | Expanded record

Expanded record · evidence type uncategorized

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

A proposed acoustofluidic blood-conditioning and monitoring architecture investigating recovery of usable blood while keeping damage, circuit state and clinician oversight explicit.

Evidence and limits

The artifact describes a control architecture and staged research program, not a tested separator, calibrated hemolysis monitor, proven alarm response or demonstrated blood-conservation benefit. Hardened and real-time labels are unsupported by measurements in that record. Its response times are design inputs, not justified clinical safety limits. This website pass reviewed saved documentation; it did not run experiments or independently validate the proposed system.
