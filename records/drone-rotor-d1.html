Summary

A civilian cargo-rotor geometry study using measured published propeller datasets to frame fair tradeoffs in twist, blade area and powertrain choices.

Evidence and limits

The archive reconstructed 31 published static propeller samples and a mass sensitivity calculation. No D1 prototype, measured improvement, rotor simulation, new patent novelty or finished aircraft was shown.
