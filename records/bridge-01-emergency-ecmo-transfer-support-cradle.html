BRIDGE-01 — Emergency ECMO transfer-support cradle

Record ID: bridge-01-emergency-ecmo-transfer-support-cradle | Expanded record

Expanded record · evidence type uncategorized

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

A proposed continuously supported transfer cradle for existing portable VA-ECMO equipment, intended to reduce tubing traction during movement between compatible carriers.

Evidence and limits

No hardware, bench, blood or human study is demonstrated. Mechanical handoff is the proposed new work; ECMO and commercial transport mounts already exist. Mechanism, interoperability, justified limits and complete resource accounting remain unresolved. It is not ready for scored qualification or human use. This website update reviewed the saved source; it did not rerun models or conduct experiments.

Contributors

Ray Peloquin with U.I. Grok and Claude reviewed the transport concept; Meta contributed to a nonclinical fixture review. Gemini withdrew unsupported numerical assumptions. None received experimental data.
