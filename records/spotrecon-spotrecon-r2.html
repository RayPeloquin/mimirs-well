Summary

A proposed pilot-supervised camera drone launching from a stationary vehicle dock to report recent parking-space observations at participating, mapped outdoor lots. The map distinguishes observed empty, occupied and unknown; an observation does not reserve a space.

Evidence and limits

Current evidence consists of illustrative calculations and structured design review. No claimed simulation result, flight test, field accuracy, docking reliability, weather qualification or regulatory approval. An ideal camera example assumes 90-degree horizontal field of view, 3840 × 2160 pixels, flat ground and a downward-looking camera at 20 m: calculated footprint 40 × 22.5 m, area 900 m², ground sampling approximately 10.4 mm/pixel. This does not establish optical resolution, classification accuracy, location error or an approved altitude. Poster observation ages are illustrative. Vehicle-specific mounting, cleared routes and reserves for return, failed approach, diversion and landing remain requirements to validate. No flight-control software or deployment package is supplied. This website update reviewed the public overview rather than running field tests.
