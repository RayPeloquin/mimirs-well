Summary

A theoretical quantum-state handoff between two stationary physical carriers through resonant local exchange and a destination phase correction. It concerns state transfer, not transport of the carriers.

Evidence and limits

Not built; no hardware demonstration. The public presentation describes theoretical average-fidelity limits under assumptions including equal rates, zero temperature and resonance. These are model claims, not measured performance. Underlying numerical-check code and data are not included in that presentation.
