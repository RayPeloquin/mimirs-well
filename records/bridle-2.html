Summary

Compare a maintainable modular aerospike architecture with BRIDLE-1, including whether added separation shifts local thermal and common-cause risks rather than eliminating them.

Evidence and limits

A normalized illustrative comparison offers a counterexample to assuming modularity is automatically cooler. It is not a calibrated temperature, hardware-safety, thrust, lifetime or reliability prediction. No apparatus or test is established.
