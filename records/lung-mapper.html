Summary

An archive title whose reviewed source proposes LUNG HARP: clinician-controlled bronchoscopic mapping of airway burden, visibility and before/after observations, with a separate clearance-support research branch.

Evidence and limits

The document is a simulation-first medical concept and proposed validation path, not measured mapping accuracy, reliable segment registration, qualified fluidics or clinical benefit. It rejects visual estimates as direct viscosity or precise obstruction measurements. Candidate dimensions, pressure, temperature and reset settings remain unvalidated design inputs. This website pass reviewed saved documentation; it did not run experiments or independently validate the proposed system.
