Summary

A proposed reproducible sandbox evaluating whether structured AI review preserves changed human permissions and useful permitted work better than simpler review under comparable resources.

Evidence and limits

The first proposed pilot uses mock notices and withdrawn dispatch permission, with common execution boundaries and separate request/dispatch/effect logs. No team appointments, funding or performance results are established. A common deterministic gate blocking every condition cannot by itself show that Court review improved safety. This website update reviewed the saved artifact; it did not rerun the underlying research.
