Summary

A proposed classical scheduling/stewardship layer for imperfect quantum infrastructure, coordinating work with calibration quality, environmental conditions and resource fairness. Recovered artwork describes classical environmental stewardship, workload scheduling and a speculative research roadmap for quantum infrastructure.

Evidence and limits

The source describes pre-execution, between-shot and calibration-window orchestration. It provides targets and a proposed validation strategy, not completed randomized hardware benchmarks, shot-efficiency improvements or validated timing. Internal fully certified wording is not evidence of independent certification. Proposed subsystem composition has not been demonstrated by the reviewed artifact. This website update reviewed the saved source; it did not rerun the research or perform hardware tests. No demonstrated improvement in coherence, fidelity, throughput, or facility reliability. Roadmap dates and numerical targets are aspirational, not predictions or results. Quantum hardware temperature requirements are platform-specific.
