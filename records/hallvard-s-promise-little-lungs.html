Summary

A neonatal respiratory-assist research aim whose current proposed step is an externally actuated diaphragm module evaluated only with artificial lungs.

Evidence and limits

Revised concept and literature-informed requirements only; no prototype or measured performance. The revision supersedes a magnetic lung-contact sleeve and rejects an assumed safe limp state after power loss. Patient interface, clinical mode, valve details, gas-contact materials and acceptance margins remain unselected or unfrozen. This website pass reviewed saved documentation; it did not run experiments or independently validate the proposed system.
