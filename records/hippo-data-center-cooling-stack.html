Summary

A proposed HIPPO architecture for coordinating data-center cooling, thermal buffering, and water stewardship. The public scope is to manage compute heat and cooling demand together, prioritize appropriate non-potable water use, and make operating conditions and resource use visible.

Evidence and limits

The selected portfolio confirms the project title. Related internal concept notes describe cooling-loop separation, thermal buffering, heat rejection, water recovery, and resource accounting as design directions. Their relationship to a single finalized stack specification remains to be established. No measured cooling capacity, water savings, power savings, uptime benefit, or operational deployment is established by the material reviewed for this public entry. A portfolio listing and concept notes are not hardware validation. Cooling moves heat; it does not eliminate the need for heat rejection. A recirculating compute loop does not establish zero water consumption across an entire facility. Any claimed reduction must be assessed against a defined baseline, operating climate, workload, and system boundary. No sizing, component selections, internal routing, control thresholds, or unpublished implementation details are included in this public record.
