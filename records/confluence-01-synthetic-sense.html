CONFLUENCE-01 — Synthetic Sense

Record ID: confluence-01-synthetic-sense | Expanded record

Expanded record · evidence type uncategorized

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

A proposed time-aware map of heat flow, magnetic fields and structural vibration, first machine-readable and then conveyed through visual or tactile cues. Initial scope is three fixed, registered zones on one guarded, isolated low-voltage motor/drive assembly.

Evidence and limits

No prototype, dataset, perceptual benefit, new biological modality or novelty has been demonstrated. The public illustration is AI-generated concept art. Proposed sensing combines a thermopile patch measuring heat flux through its own surface, contextual surface temperature, a fixed three-axis magnetic sensor and a rigidly mounted triaxial accelerometer. Contact, mounting and unequal response times affect interpretation. The map covers instrumented zones, not unsensed space. Proposed states are quiet, familiar operation, thermal after-effect, unfamiliar change and unavailable. Conflicting valid data is not a fault diagnosis. Quiet is not an all-clear. Multisensor monitoring and sensory augmentation have precedents. The research question is whether this representation improves discrimination and attention demands against simpler alternatives. No improvement is established; this website update reviewed the public plan, not measured outcomes.
