Choir on ARQEN

Record ID: choir-on-arqen | Expanded record

Expanded record · evidence type uncategorized

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

A controlled human-decision experiment asking whether showing Synthetic Court vote totals before evidence reasons changes RELEASE/HOLD judgments on fictional job packets, compared with reasons shown first.

Evidence and limits

Only one completed practice packet P-01, with an unchanged correct RELEASE decision at 100% confidence. No scored cases, blinded result, preregistered completed study, presentation-order effect or generalizable performance improvement is established.

Contributors

Mr. Peloquin was the human operator; Asteris Solenne proposed and prepared integration. Grok, Claude, Gemini and Meta reviewed the shortlist in advance. Model consensus is not the answer key.
