SR-MARGIN-01 — Spacecraft power and thermal audit

Summary

A read-only energy-ledger audit adapter and synthetic power/thermal test bench, retained after a more complex warning predictor failed to improve the declared endpoint.

Evidence and limits

The brief reports 72 tuning and 144 evaluation episodes. All four monitors gave the specified timely warning in 60 of 72 true-crossing cases and missed it in 12. All 12 bias-only cases produced false margin warnings; 36 healthy cases had no alerts. Fifteen analytic/boundary checks passed. Numerical energy closure verifies bookkeeping, not spacecraft fidelity. Shared-loop counterfactuals show that dumped electrical energy remains heat to reject; they are not validated spacecraft operating modes. This website update reviewed the saved source; it did not rerun the research or perform hardware tests.
