Summary

A proposed portable decision-support pattern using local existing signals, uncertainty-aware fusion and attention/resource constraints at the point of need.

Evidence and limits

The source lists candidate carriers and applications, but reports no working mobile host, field trial, battery/bandwidth result or measured decision benefit. Commercial-ready and rapid-deployment assertions are not adopted as proven capability. This website update reviewed the saved artifact; it did not rerun the underlying research.
