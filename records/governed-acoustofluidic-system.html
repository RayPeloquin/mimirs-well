Summary

A fluid-processing research architecture combining acoustic separation, sensing and bounded control, with industrial and extracorporeal clinical applications requiring separate validation.

Evidence and limits

The source supplies proposed layers, failure responses and development phases; no measured separation efficiency, contaminant removal, blood compatibility or implemented protection is reported. Universal minimum-flow and timeout assertions are not justified across patients or circuits by this artifact and are not adopted as safety limits. This website pass reviewed saved documentation; it did not run experiments or independently validate the proposed system.
