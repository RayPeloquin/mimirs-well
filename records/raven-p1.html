RAVEN-P1

Summary

A 92-residue computational sequence redesign for the published Top7 structural scaffold. Work comprises a computational candidate and forward structure prediction; no new laboratory synthesis or testing is reported.

Evidence and limits

The public README describes eight computational structure predictions spanning Top7, RAVEN-P1 and six intermediate sequences. Its forward-structure figure reports C-alpha RMSD to experimental Top7 of 0.80 Å for the Top7 reference prediction and 0.78 Å for RAVEN-P1. These are computational structural comparisons, not measurements of a synthesized RAVEN-P1 protein. The model-score table contains 97 analysis records. Saved sequence/structural verification, provenance and environment files are linked; this website update did not rerun those checks. Scripts, dependencies, remaining reference inputs and remaining outputs are pending upload. The study cannot yet be reproduced from repository contents alone. No binding, catalysis, therapeutic effect, solubility, stability or safety property is established. This is not a claim of a new fold, worldwide sequence novelty or experimentally verified folding.

Contributors

Mr. Peloquin commissioned the work and set its computer-only scope. Asteris Solenne / ChatGPT designed and executed the computational study and prepared its artifacts. AI-assisted outside review is advisory, not experimental validation. Original scaffold, model and software authors retain attribution: published Top7 (Kuhlman et al., Science, 2003; PDB 1QYS), ProteinMPNN and OmegaFold.
