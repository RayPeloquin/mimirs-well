HIVE-1 — Human–AI cognitive network

Record ID: hive-1-human-ai-cognitive-network | Expanded record

Expanded record · evidence type uncategorized

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

A proposed voluntary human–AI task network that collects deliberate answers, preserves independent first responses, exposes disagreement and compares coordinated work against ordinary teams and AI-only baselines.

Evidence and limits

No prototype, human trial, neural enhancement, pooled processor capacity or commercial advantage is demonstrated. Behavioral participation is the primary design; optional read-only EEG research is a separate unvalidated branch. Illustrative scaling arithmetic is not a measure of combined intelligence or observed participant performance. This website update reviewed the saved artifact; it did not rerun the underlying research.
