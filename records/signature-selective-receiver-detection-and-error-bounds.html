Summary

An analytic and synthetic reference connecting declared waveform/interference uncertainty to a receiver statistic, conditional error bound and received-energy requirement.

Evidence and limits

The archived note reports 27 synthetic checks. In its constructed example, robust receiver predicted worst conditional BER is 0.20905%, compared with 0.21300% simulated. Each receiver is tested at its own least favorable admitted disturbance, not a common field environment. The result depends on the declared geometry and noise model. Gaussian tail guarantees do not follow from covariance alone. No prototype, circuit reconstruction or measured supply-efficiency advantage is established. This website update reviewed the saved source; it did not rerun the research or perform hardware tests.

Contributors

Prepared for Ray Peloquin. The note distinguishes its calculations from established robust-filtering literature and Nikfal, Zhang and Caloz's phaser work; it does not reconstruct their undisclosed receiver.
