Summary

A proposed inter-layer communication and coordination architecture for keeping state freshness, disagreement, fallback behavior and recovery decisions explicit in physical systems.

Evidence and limits

The source gives timing, thermal and coordination targets with proposed failure responses, not measured communication latency, qualified materials, false-isolation reduction or integrated survivability. Confidence-driven emergency authority proposals need reconciliation with uncertainty-based authority limits. A component clock does not establish end-to-end decision latency. This website pass reviewed saved documentation; it did not rerun the underlying system or conduct physical/clinical experiments.
