Summary

VEIL-E / EDISON-01, the Quiet Receiver, proposes a paired torsion-balance experiment to measure and challenge reproducible weak physical signals under blinded controls. It separates anomaly detection, information transfer and any claim of source identity.

Evidence and limits

The eight-page archived review provides apparatus proposals, known-force calibration and artifact tests, not new measurements. No receiver built, no sensitivity measured, no repeatable anomaly found, no communication demonstrated and no established physical coupling to deceased consciousness.

Contributors
