Summary

CHRONOVISOR–N/S Ghostglass is a synthetic on/off counting reference for checking statistical alerts and a traceable review protocol in a neutrino-research context. It compares background treatments and multiple-testing correction using simulated counts. It is not a demonstrated neutrino detector.

Evidence and limits

Saved software test record: 15 tests passed. This website update reviewed the committed record; it did not rerun the suite. Committed simulation plan: seed 20261019; 20,000 families; 100 trials per family; alpha 0.01; mean on-background 0.2; off/on exposure ratio 10; injected signal mean 6. Null family false-alert rates: uncorrected known background 10.605%; Bonferroni known background 0.590%; Bonferroni with naive estimated background 91.695%; Bonferroni exact on/off 0.105% (95% Clopper–Pearson interval 0.0650%–0.1605%). These are separate procedures under the committed simulation settings, not detector performance ratings. Injected-window power: 65.625% (95% interval 64.962%–66.283%). Unflagged rate-shift stress: 32.445% alert rate. Unflagged overdispersion stress: 50.925%. These stress results expose failures when model assumptions are violated. Assumptions: fixed windows and cuts; stationary equal on/off rates; independent on/off Poisson counts; full exposure; trusted quality metadata. Bonferroni requires valid marginal p-values, not independence between test windows. Limits: per-family false-alert probability, not yearly false-alarm rate or astrophysical discovery significance. No real observations, full particle/light transport, or spatial likelihood. No blind human custody of the synthetic data. Hardware remains unvalidated.
