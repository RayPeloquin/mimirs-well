Horizon Glasses

Record ID: horizon-glasses | Expanded record

Expanded record · evidence type uncategorized

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

A motion-comfort research concept exploring whether a subtle peripheral reference cue adds useful information without adding visual distraction or sensory conflict.

Evidence and limits

The artifact supplies design reasoning and a proposed perception/control comparison, not a tested prototype, symptom reduction or proven superiority to obstruction/placebo cues. A fluid interface responds to apparent gravity including acceleration; it is not an unconditional Earth-horizontal reference in a moving vehicle. Claimed instantaneous response and all-orientation benefit are not established. This website pass reviewed saved documentation; it did not run experiments or independently validate the proposed system.

Contributors

The source attributes vision to Mr. Peloquin and systems work to Asteris; independent perception and human-factors contributions remain unverified.
