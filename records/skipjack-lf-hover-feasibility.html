Summary

Explore a purpose-built uncrewed rotorcraft for moving inert cargo across a short, difficult ground gap, compared with ordinary nonflight alternatives.

Evidence and limits

The archived rotorcraft sizing examples depend on assumed performance. No closed mass/power bill, tested propulsion system, measured rotor outwash, safe fault recovery, aviation permission or flight is established.
