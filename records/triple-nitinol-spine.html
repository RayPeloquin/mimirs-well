Triple-Nitinol Spine

Record ID: triple-nitinol-spine | Expanded record

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

A proposed three-column nickel-titanium flexure architecture, illustrated with distinct load, torsion and recovery roles. First study is a nonbiological mechanical model.

Evidence and limits

Concept renderings establish intended geometry, not measured load sharing, actuation, fatigue, or durability. Shape-memory transformation and superelastic recovery have different operating conditions; recovery alone does not establish service life. A three-column geometry could increase mass or stress concentrations. No prototype, implant suitability or human testing established. Proposed test only: compare a three-column model and conventional flexure at equal mass, boundary conditions and load envelope; measure displacement, peak modeled strain and torsional stiffness, including material-model sensitivity. Reject a claimed improvement if it fails to outperform the baseline on the preregistered target while meeting the other constraints. Model convergence is required; fatigue life remains a separate question.
