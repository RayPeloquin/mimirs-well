EMH — Field Guide Gloves

Record ID: emh-field-guide-gloves | Expanded record

Expanded record · evidence type uncategorized

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

EMH FIELD explores lightweight sensing and tactile cues to help an appropriately trained person perform an approved simulated field-care task using their own hands. The v0.1 glove is advisory, not powered or autonomous.

Evidence and limits

Design requirements and fault-challenge plan only. No glove prototype, tested interface, calibrated sensing, measured benefit, universal suitability, clinical safety or patient-use competence is established. Training-manikin work is the first proposed scope.

Contributors
