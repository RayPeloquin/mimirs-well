The Traveler — Solar-electric research airship

Summary

A civilian solar-electric stratospheric airship concept that would ride winds around Earth and return within reach of a staffed recovery site.

Evidence and limits

Twelve passive-drift runs produced five longitude laps and three post-lap approaches within 500 km. Two cases ended at the polar limit. Descent, physical recovery and airspace authorization remain untested. No aircraft or camera hardware was flown.
