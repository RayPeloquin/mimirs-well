GO-CUBE — Liberty Field Governor

Record ID: go-cube-liberty-field-governor | Expanded record

Concept or design review

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Evidence type describes the public artifact, not a validation or safety certificate.

Summary

A proposed supervisory decision layer that evaluates whether an increase in authority, commitment or resource use leaves protected recovery options. The Chutes Cube considers ascent; GO-CUBE considers continued recoverability.

Evidence and limits

The public artwork and documentation describe proposed territory, pressure and liberty states, a Two-Eye Rule and a Liberty Index. Illustrated thresholds and scores are not measurements or safety guarantees. Two nominal recovery paths may share a common failure domain, and a scalar score cannot substitute for hard authority and safety constraints. The separate Hypha Mind / GO-CUBE authenticated-update pilot does not validate this governor. No integrated implementation, calibrated scoring, deployment, or independent validation is documented. Proposed test only: on frozen synthetic state graphs, compare a permission-only baseline with a recovery-aware rule at equal request budgets; measure accepted actions with no surviving authorized exit, false refusals, and common-cause failures. Reject the recovery claim if the rule accepts a state with no protected reachable exit under the stated fault model or fails to improve the baseline at a declared refusal budget.
