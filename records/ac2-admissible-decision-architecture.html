AC² — Admissible decision architecture

Summary

A proposed decision-review architecture for making disagreement, uncertainty and authority limits explicit. The reviewed integration example uses AC² as a parallel contradiction monitor rather than the sole permission gate.

Evidence and limits

The audit resolves a proposed fast-path/monitoring-role ambiguity but leaves implementation information flow and empirical validation pending. Two checkers can agree incorrectly or create denial of service. Internal review colors and acceptance of a plan are not measurements of contradiction detection, timing or safe recovery. This website pass reviewed saved documentation; it did not rerun the underlying system or conduct physical/clinical experiments.

Contributors

Kimi tribunal commentary is recorded in the archived integration audit; individual implementation ownership remains unverified.

Source revision

An ATCB-01 v0.5 integration audit describes AC²'s intended monitoring role; a standalone general AC² specification was not verified. Other AC² applications require separate scope/version reconciliation. Source reviewed November 17, 2026.
