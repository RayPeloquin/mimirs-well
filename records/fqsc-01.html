Summary

Field-Quench Spectral Coupling (FQSC-01) is a simulation-first study of how a time-varying electromagnetic field exchanges energy with a confined atomic ion. The proposed measurement separates internal electronic excitation from motion of the ion as a whole. The illustrated apparatus is a concept for future discussion, not working laboratory equipment.

Evidence and limits

Trapped-ion confinement, optical transitions and motional control are established atomic-physics methods, not discoveries demonstrated by FQSC-01. The current FQSC-01 proposal contains an AI-generated instrument image, an outline of components and a simulation plan. No physical device, instrument measurements, validated numerical predictions, novel excitation mechanism, nuclear disintegration, or LHC replacement is established. The proposed field-quench effect must be evaluated against standard spectral-overlap and energy–momentum constraints before assigning any new capability.
