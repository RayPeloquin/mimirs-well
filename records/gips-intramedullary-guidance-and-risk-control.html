Summary

A proposed surgeon-controlled intramedullary guidance and risk-control platform, combining corridor visualization, uncertainty, neuromonitoring context and refusal of inadequately supported actions.

Evidence and limits

The reviewed document states proposed update rules, intervention tiers and logging requirements, but supplies no hardware, bench or clinical validation report. Its adult-validation wording is unsupported by evidence in that artifact and is not adopted here. Numerical intervention thresholds and performance assertions are unvalidated design inputs. This website update reviewed the saved source; it did not rerun models or conduct experiments.
