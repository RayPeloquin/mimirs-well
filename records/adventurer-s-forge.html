Summary

An AI-assisted tabletop RPG concept in which exploration, invention and technical reasoning become part of the adventure. Human players choose their actions; the proposed Game Master, Physics Referee, Technology Compendium and Campaign Journal support play within declared constraints.

Evidence and limits

Rules and the opening Broken Skybridge scene are written. The published validation note reports desk checks of conditional cushion, central-point cable, winch-energy and heat-engine examples. Those calculations do not establish imagined energy sources or validate real hardware. The October 29 publication check reports working relative document/image links, four decodable selected artworks, manifest agreement and preservation of current v0.3 and archived v0.2 packets. This website update reviewed those saved claims; it did not rerun the publication audit. No completed human playtest, running game application, integrated companion service, enforced familiar permissions, adversarial access tests or automatic persistence is reported. No hardware validation, completed D&D/SRD adapter or established uniqueness claim.
