Summary

A research concept for focused-ultrasound-assisted drug delivery in adult recurrent glioblastoma, using an existing research platform and a traceable margin-planning workflow.

Evidence and limits

No prototype, simulation result, clinical performance or novelty is claimed. Published blood–brain-barrier opening and drug-delivery studies motivate investigation but do not validate this proposed combination. More drug delivery or MRI enhancement does not itself establish tumor control or survival benefit. This website update reviewed the saved source; it did not rerun models or conduct experiments.
