Summary

Evidence and limits

The addendum describes separating measurements, equations, models, interpretations and metaphors, and declaring observations that would challenge a claim. It is a review method, not a new physical theory or evidence of better engineering outcomes. Internal approval labels do not establish independent validation. This website update reviewed saved documentation; it did not rerun models or conduct experiments. Artwork review: The artwork expresses a research method. Decorative equations do not establish a derivation, novel theory or independently replicated result.
