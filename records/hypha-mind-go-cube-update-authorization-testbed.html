Summary

A toy registry and authorization-interface study comparing full reconstruction with authenticated local update witnesses.

Evidence and limits

The reviewed report preserves the failed submitted v0.1 run separately from the corrected local v0.2 pilot. The frozen pilot reports 456 scenario rows, 18 commit controls and 640 agreeing benchmark pairs. Shared controller and validation code limits independence. The witness route does not immediately detect arbitrary backing-store corruption. Hash-work ratios are not elapsed-time ratios; instrumented timing is not a production guarantee. No learning-quality, complete GO-CUBE-governor, action-safety or fundamental-time result is established. This website update reviewed the saved source; it did not rerun the research or perform hardware tests.
