Summary

A proposed photoelectric electron-source concept. Separate photon-driven emission from externally supplied downstream electron acceleration and from total energy-conversion efficiency.

Evidence and limits

In the elementary single-photon photoelectric model, maximum initial electron kinetic energy is K_max = hf - phi above threshold, with h Planck's constant, f frequency and phi work function. K_max divided by electron rest energy is a dimensionless normalized energy, not an efficiency. An accelerating voltage contributes energy from a separate supply. The artwork does not establish electron yield, source current, conversion efficiency or an operating apparatus. Proposed test only: perform a dimensionally consistent calculation across the emission threshold, compare with the elementary photoelectric model, and account separately for external electrical input. Reject any energy-gain or efficiency claim that lacks measured or explicitly modeled input/output accounting. No hardware test is proposed in this review.
