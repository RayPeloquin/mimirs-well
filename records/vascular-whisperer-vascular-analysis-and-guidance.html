Summary

A proposed clinician-facing vascular-imaging research workflow that flags absence, asymmetry and tissue consequences for review, with particular interest in missed distal/medium-vessel occlusions.

Evidence and limits

The source describes a detection/adjudication architecture and proposed retrospective-to-prospective evaluation path. It contains no trained-model artifact, dataset results, diagnostic accuracy or demonstrated false-negative recovery. Adjudication targets and workflow benefits are not observed outcomes. This website update reviewed the saved source; it did not rerun models or conduct experiments.
