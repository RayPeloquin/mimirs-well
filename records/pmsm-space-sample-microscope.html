PMSM — Space sample microscope

Record ID: pmsm-space-sample-microscope | Expanded record

Standard public record; no technical-release approval implied. Publication policy

Summary

A proposed portable multi-mode sample microscope and a space-workstation adaptation intended for a pressurized workspace. The initial design comparison concerns imaging, sample stability and power.

Evidence and limits

The two plates describe concepts and proposed specifications, not measured optical performance, battery life or flight qualification. A pressurized workspace is not necessarily a microgravity-qualified environment; illumination, sample retention, vibration, optical alignment and power must be treated separately. Proposed test only: compare a modeled candidate and conventional portable microscope on the same synthetic calibration targets and declared photon/power budget; quantify resolution, contrast, simulated sample displacement and energy per usable image. Reject a claimed optical or energy advantage if it disappears under matched conditions or depends on an unstated sample-stability assumption. No hardware or flight test performed.
