Summary

A proposed human-readable role/interface contract for AI collaboration, keeping continuity of presentation distinct from actual permissions, evidence and tool authority.

Evidence and limits

The source states an intended separation between user-facing role and backend enforcement. It does not demonstrate a complete deployed boundary, injection resistance, persistent identity service or measured trust/usability benefit. Persona consistency is not proof of authority or correctness. This website update reviewed saved documentation; it did not rerun models or conduct experiments.
