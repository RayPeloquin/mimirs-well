HEMOSTAT-R · Two kinds of stopping need different tests.

01 / The three questions

Keep intent, evidence and hypothesis apart.

01 / INTENDED

What we intended

Consider a temporary trauma-repair scaffold with locally bounded activity and an explicit transition to material removal or clearance while preserving necessary structural support.

A goal is not an experimental result.

02 / DOCUMENTED

What actually happened

The reviewed summary reports no selected material class, working control pathway, bleeding-control data or verified clearance. Its critical distinction is between stopping new activity and removing a structure that might still support a seal.

This describes the available source record; no new tests are implied.

03 / POSSIBLE CROSSOVER

What else might this be useful for?

A nonclinical material-lifecycle benchmark could separately measure active effects, load-bearing integrity and material persistence. It is a proposed assessment framework, not a clinical treatment.

New hypothesis only; independent validation needed.

Evidence boundary: No material recipe, hemorrhage-control efficacy, emergency use or approved treatment.
02 / Next reproducible brick

What would change the record?

1 · Preserve

Choose a harmless nonclinical surrogate and observation period.

2 · Measure

Specify distinct activity, mechanical support and persistence endpoints.

3 · Challenge

Verify material fate independently before considering any biological interpretation.
