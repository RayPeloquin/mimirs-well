OFF is not empty.

ECHO-Rx · A modeled shutoff still has a tail.

What we intended. What actually happened. What else this might be useful for. A design review or paper calculation is not a physical experiment.

01 / The three questions

Keep intent, evidence and hypothesis apart.

01 / INTENDED

What we intended

Study a contained cell-based system that responds to one declared inflammatory input and produces one active protein, while measuring both its function and what remains after production stops.

A goal is not an experimental result.

02 / DOCUMENTED

What actually happened

The public repository contains a reduced two-compartment mathematical shutdown model, not an ECHO-Rx biological result. Its normalized demonstration peaks at target level 1.2805 after the idealized OFF event, at time 1.7028, and integrates to a tail of 11. Analytical and RK4 calculations agree within the reported model checks. These are arbitrary units, not dose or clearance measurements.

This describes the available source record; no new tests are implied.

03 / POSSIBLE CROSSOVER

What else might this be useful for?

Use the residual-tail model as a general audit question for systems that stop producing new output but retain stored or delayed effects. That is a candidate benchmark, not a demonstrated therapy or a validated shutdown.

New hypothesis only; independent validation needed.

Evidence boundary: No cell system or clinical result. OFF, protein clearance, carrier persistence and tissue recovery are distinct endpoints.
02 / Next reproducible brick

What would change the record?

1 · Preserve

Reproduce the saved model and clearly state its assumptions.

2 · Measure

Compare delayed transfer, residual activity and state change as separate outcomes using safe analogues.

3 · Challenge

Require independent biological and tissue-effect evidence before any treatment interpretation.
