Which change in tile sound deserves a second look?

A change in sound gives the mason a place to inspect. Joints and backing materials can produce changes too.

What we intended

What the source documents

The public digest describes comparison areas selected by the operator. It reports no calibrated map or false-positive rate.

Possible crossover

Inspectors of other tiled surfaces could use the same distinction between locating an anomaly and diagnosing its cause.

Proposed next test

Have an independent reviewer compare blinded calls against documented backing conditions, including intact joints. Count false flags and missed changes separately.
