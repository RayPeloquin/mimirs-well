01 / The three questions

Keep intent, evidence and hypothesis apart.

01 / INTENDED

What we intended

Investigate temporal reflection of an RF pulse in a rapidly switched transmission line. The proposed eight-foot conductive room is optional workspace and shielding, not itself a time-varying medium.

A goal is not an experimental result.

02 / DOCUMENTED

What actually happened

The repository reports ideal transmission-line frequency, velocity and timing calculations, including the conditional reversed-arrival relation. No chamber or switched line has been tested, and no temporal-reflection signature or energy balance has been measured. Ordinary reflections, mixing and switching artifacts remain competing interpretations.

This describes the available source record; no new tests are implied.

03 / POSSIBLE CROSSOVER

What else might this be useful for?

Even a null result could make this into an RF pulse-metrology testbed for measuring switch artifacts, propagation delays and calibration drift. That is a future possible use.

New hypothesis only; independent validation needed.

Evidence boundary: No stopped time, time travel, permanent broadcast storage or net energy gain. A conductive room alone has no temporal interface.
02 / Next reproducible brick

What would change the record?
