01 / The three questions

Keep intent, evidence and hypothesis apart.

01 / INTENDED

What we intended

Describe a human-authorized unarmed observation quadruped for permitted terrain and environmental tasks, with bounded stopping and transparent operator controls.

A goal is not an experimental result.

02 / DOCUMENTED

What actually happened

The public note contains a requirements critique and concept images, not chosen hardware, measured sensing, validated stops or field results. A wolf-like shell is an identity choice, not evidence of higher performance.

This describes the available source record; no new tests are implied.

03 / POSSIBLE CROSSOVER

What else might this be useful for?

The constraints could be adapted for low-risk supervised bridge or remote-infrastructure observation, with uncertainty and privacy reported explicitly. That possibility remains untested.

New hypothesis only; independent validation needed.

Evidence boundary: No pursuit, person identification, targeting or combat. Court critique is not hardware approval.
02 / Next reproducible brick

What would change the record?

1 · Preserve

Pick an ordinary nonhazardous platform and permitted test area.

2 · Measure

Measure stopping response, operator handoff and location uncertainty.

3 · Challenge

Keep human observation boundaries explicit, without person-tracking or combat features.
