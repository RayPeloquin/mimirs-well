The Crucible: experimental work queue

Research cycle map: start here or find the next stage.

Turn a research question into a bounded, falsifiable test. This work queue is distinct from the CRUCIBLE autonomy research project. All six entries are proposed test plans, not results. Later observed executions are indexed separately in the Chronicle. Priority 1 = small arithmetic fixtures; 2 = additional assumptions or implementation; 3 = protocol development required. No physical tests are authorized.

Machine-readable plans | Download the offline test worksheet | Research relationships | Publication boundaries.

Research workflow

  1. Choose a plan and read its source and limits.
  2. Freeze the plan, source revision, environment, implementation, fixture and acceptance rules before seeing outcomes. Export a dated plan receipt.
  3. Execute independently, outside this tool, only within the synthetic boundary.
  4. Record actual observations, artifacts, deviations and outcome; retain failures and inconclusive cases. Export a separate reported-run receipt for review. A completed worksheet is not proof that a run happened.

Priority 1 | Proposed plan

Catch missing material in a closed synthetic ledger

Does explicit residual accounting distinguish an incomplete ledger from a complete one?

Zion Twin-Ring Tuna Habitat · SpinCell DLE | Source question | Ledger: SpinCell DLE

Chronicle: Original fixture receipt | Expanded fixture receipt

Equipment
Existing computer; Node.js or a spreadsheet. No material, water or animals.
Estimated incremental cost
No additional equipment or paid data expected.
Effort
Small deterministic fixture; proposed, not timed.
Baseline
Naive product/input ratio that ignores missing outlets or inventory.
Fixture / protocol
Use three normalized ledgers with input 100 units: closed (product 60, outlet 25, inventory increase 15); omitted outlet (product 60, outlet omitted, inventory increase 15); omitted inventory (product 60, outlet 25, inventory omitted). Missing quantities are unknown, not zero.
Metric
Residual = input minus known product, known outlet and recorded inventory change; report missing-field flag separately.
Acceptance
Closed fixture residual 0; omitted-outlet fixture apparent residual 25 and missing-field flag; omitted-inventory fixture apparent residual 15 and missing-field flag. Naive product ratio 0.60 in all three. All must match.
Contrary result
Any wrong residual or acceptance of an incomplete ledger as closed rejects the proposed check on these fixtures.
Stop / inconclusive
Stop as inconclusive if inputs, units or expected missing-field labels differ from the frozen fixture.
Scope and safety boundary
Only deterministic arithmetic on synthetic normalized numbers. No tracer, extraction or habitat performance.

Priority 1 | Proposed plan

Expose a misleading failure denominator

Can per-run fractions and per-hour rates reveal different failure patterns?

CRUCIBLE · CONFLUENCE-01 — Synthetic Sense | Source question | Ledger: CRUCIBLE

Chronicle: Original fixture receipt | Expanded fixture receipt

Equipment
Existing computer; Node.js or spreadsheet.
Estimated incremental cost
No additional equipment expected.
Effort
Small deterministic fixture; proposed, not timed.
Baseline
Compare groups using only failed-run fraction.
Fixture / protocol
Synthetic groups A and B each have 10 conclusive runs, 2 failed runs and 2 counted events; valid observation durations are 10 and 20 hours respectively. C has 10 conclusive runs, 4 failed runs, 4 events, 20 valid hours. D has zero valid hours. Event counts are stipulated separately, not inferred from run failure.
Metric
Failed runs/conclusive runs; events/valid hours, with denominator and undefined values visible.
Acceptance
A and B both 0.2 failed-run fraction but rates 0.2 and 0.1 events/hour; A and C both 0.2 events/hour but fractions 0.2 and 0.4. D rate undefined, never zero.
Contrary result
Conflated denominators, a numeric zero for undefined rate, or an incorrect ratio rejects the check.
Stop / inconclusive
Stop as inconclusive if scenario coverage or valid durations cannot be reconstructed.
Scope and safety boundary
Synthetic bookkeeping only. No safety or autonomy performance.

Priority 1 | Proposed plan

Detect double counting and an impossible ideal heat budget

Do independent accounting and ideal ceiling checks detect different errors?

Chihuahua Sphere / CS-1 · Adventurer’s Forge | Source question | Ledger: Chihuahua Sphere / CS-1

Chronicle: Original fixture receipt | Expanded fixture receipt

Equipment
Existing computer; arithmetic or spreadsheet.
Estimated incremental cost
No additional equipment expected.
Effort
Small deterministic fixture; proposed, not timed.
Baseline
Naively sum all reported transfers as useful output.
Fixture / protocol
Synthetic input 100 energy units, terminal electricity 30, terminal rejected heat 70. Separate hypothetical heat-engine case: hot 400 K, cold 300 K, heat input 20 units; compare work 5 versus 6. Deliberate ledger error counts the 30 intermediate transfer again.
Metric
Terminal ledger closure and independent ideal Carnot ceiling 1 - Tcold/Thot.
Acceptance
Correct terminal ledger closes at 100; double-counted ledger sums to 130 and is flagged. Separate ideal heat-engine ceiling 0.25; work 5 at the ceiling is not rejected by that bound, work 6 is refused by it.
Contrary result
Double counting passes, the 6-unit case passes the ceiling, or the 5-unit case is described as demonstrated performance.
Stop / inconclusive
Stop as inconclusive if boundaries or absolute-temperature assumptions change.
Scope and safety boundary
Synthetic resource accounting only; no apparatus, thermal design or energy-generation claim.

Priority 2 | Proposed plan

Check observation-age labels against synthetic timestamps

Can a freshness rule reject stale observations without hiding unavailable intervals?

SPOTRECON / SPOTRECON R2 · CONFLUENCE-01 — Synthetic Sense | Source question

No observed run for this plan is recorded in the Chronicle.

Equipment
Existing computer; scripted synthetic timestamp table.
Estimated incremental cost
No external sensors or data expected.
Effort
Small synthetic table; proposed, not timed.
Baseline
Age-blind display that accepts every present observation.
Fixture / protocol
At display time 100 s, capture times 99, 95, 94 and unknown. Prespecify maximum age 5 s inclusive, common clock and missing-time rejection. Include a future capture at 101 s as invalid.
Metric
Accepted, stale, missing and invalid labels; coverage = accepted observations/all 5 cases.
Acceptance
Ages 1 and 5 accepted; age 6 stale; unknown missing; future invalid. Coverage 2/5; no unavailable observation is presented as verified.
Contrary result
Any stale, missing or future observation is accepted, or unavailable cases disappear from the denominator.
Stop / inconclusive
Stop as inconclusive if the timestamps do not share a clock or the inclusive boundary was changed.
Scope and safety boundary
Synthetic timestamps, not camera, drone or field reliability.

Priority 2 | Proposed plan

Separate numerical guards from physical claims

Can a calculation enforce its stated ideal stability bound without certifying a model?

SKEPTIC-1 · Temporal Acoustics | Source question | Ledger: SKEPTIC-1 | Ledger: Temporal Acoustics

No observed run for this plan is recorded in the Chronicle.

Equipment
Existing computer; arithmetic and published source definitions.
Estimated incremental cost
No additional equipment expected.
Effort
Small numerical guard fixture; proposed, not timed.
Baseline
Accept every positive dimensionless Courant value without checking the stated bound.
Fixture / protocol
For the stated ideal equal-spacing 2-D scalar-wave guard compare dimensionless values 0.5, 1/sqrt(2), and 0.8. Independently record the SKEPTIC source as a different ideal model, not a coupled equation.
Metric
Guard decision against inclusive 1/sqrt(2), with exact boundary comparison or declared numerical tolerance.
Acceptance
0.5 and exact boundary permitted by the stated guard; 0.8 refused. Report no inference about physical accuracy or SKEPTIC coupling.
Contrary result
0.8 accepted, boundary handled inconsistently, or guard acceptance called physical validation.
Stop / inconclusive
Stop if source assumptions or comparison tolerance are not fixed before the check.
Scope and safety boundary
Arithmetic guard only; not a convergence study or physical experiment.

Priority 3 | Proposed plan

Design a blinded independent-signal comparison

Can a separately generated quality flag improve false-alert discrimination at matched sensitivity?

Ghostglass · Chihuahua Sphere / CS-1 | Source question | Ledger: Ghostglass | Ledger: Chihuahua Sphere / CS-1

No observed run for this plan is recorded in the Chronicle.

Equipment
Existing computer; synthetic data generator and independently checked evaluation script.
Estimated incremental cost
No external data or equipment expected; implementation effort unknown.
Effort
Protocol development required before execution.
Baseline
Statistics-only and conventional quality-control arms at matched sensitivity.
Fixture / protocol
Before any run, freeze independent null/injected-signal generator, residual-generation mechanism, calibration and held-out sets, random seeds, arm definitions, sample size and uncertainty method. No parameter selection on held-out outcomes.
Metric
Held-out false-alert rate, sensitivity, inconclusive fraction and uncertainty for each arm.
Acceptance
Proposed acceptance rule: predeclare a positive minimum false-alert improvement, sensitivity equivalence margin and uncertainty method; require the specified interval criteria against both baselines and report coverage. No execution-ready numeric threshold yet.
Contrary result
No improvement under frozen criteria, sensitivity loss beyond margin, or residual computed circularly from the same counts.
Stop / inconclusive
Do not execute until numerical thresholds, sample size, independent generator and blinded analysis plan are reviewed and frozen.
Scope and safety boundary
Synthetic statistics only. No detector, optical hardware or sensitivity claims.
