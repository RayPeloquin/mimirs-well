# Hydrofell-01 — Hydro data center

A hydro-powered computing facility would house servers in a dry vault, circulate coolant through heat exchangers and offer recovered heat to nearby users. The illustration spells the name Hydrofall-01.

## Evidence

The illustrated readouts provide no operating history, flood assessment or evidence of a year-round heat customer.

## Proposed next check

Compare hourly compute demand and heat output with available electricity and a prospective heat user’s seasonal demand.
