# Off-Grid Ranch Water System — Atmospheric collection and trough delivery

**Status:** Illustrated livestock-water concept; no site-specific yield or field deployment.

## Public purpose

The Off-Grid Ranch Water System catalog entry concerns a modular way to collect water from humid air, store it and supply livestock troughs with a straightforward human-maintainable delivery arrangement. A matching archive illustration is headed **“SKY TROUGH v0”** and combines passive overnight collection, optional daytime powered collection, storage and level monitoring in one picture.

That visual is a concept of possible functions, not proof they supply enough water for animals in a given climate. The link between the catalog title and the SKY TROUGH artwork is topical; formal version lineage has not been verified.

## Evidence limits

No measured water yield, year-round reliability, treated-water quality, potable suitability, animal-welfare performance, off-grid energy balance, installed costs or working ranch system is established. Daily output would depend on local humidity, temperature, collection surface and actual power. Continuous availability is an untested aspiration from the illustration.

## Next check

Choose a real climate and herd-water requirement, compare conventional supply/storage alternatives, then quantify collection, energy, water testing, sanitation, maintenance and drought-season failure cases. Keep animal-care decisions with qualified humans.
