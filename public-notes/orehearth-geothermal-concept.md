# OREHEARTH — Geothermal concept

A circulating fluid would carry heat from rock near an ore body to a surface heat exchanger while leaving the ore underground.

## Evidence

The artwork depicts both a sealed loop and flow through fractures. The fluid boundaries differ, and the poster supplies no measured net output.

## Proposed next check

Draw separate fluid boundaries for the two configurations and compare available heat with pumping demand.
