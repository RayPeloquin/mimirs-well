# NDSM-01 — Neutrino feasibility study

**Status:** Paper feasibility analysis, v0.1; no apparatus, tested conversion or external Court approval.

## Public purpose

NDSM-01 asks whether known neutrino–nucleus interactions could support a *tagged and statistically observed* conversion event in a defined target. The reviewed research separates this bounded measurement question from the much stronger ambition of picking one chosen atom and altering it on command.

The archived work concludes that a useful atom-by-atom editing device is unsupported by the rates and control mechanisms it considered. Its defensible continuation is to model a reaction channel and the event signature that an existing experimental facility might be able to distinguish from background.

## Evidence limits

No functioning neutrino manipulator, focused neutrino lens, single-atom addressability, predicted practical production yield, custom detector validation or independent Court review is established. Example rates in the source use favorable stated assumptions and cannot be advertised as achievable device performance. The distinction between statistical conversion and selected-atom control must remain explicit.

## Next check

Request specialist review of the candidate reaction, published cross-sections, source spectrum, detection efficiency and backgrounds. Reproduce the statistical benchmark against primary nuclear data before considering a facility proposal.
