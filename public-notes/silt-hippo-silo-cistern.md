# Silt HIPPO — Silo cistern

The design proposes lining a reused silo, collecting sediment at a low point and drawing water above that layer. A bottom purge would remove settled solids.

## Evidence

The schematic establishes neither structural suitability for water storage nor drinking-water quality.

## Proposed next check

Use a transparent model with inert sediment. Measure solids in the draw-off and purge streams and weigh what remains after flushing.
