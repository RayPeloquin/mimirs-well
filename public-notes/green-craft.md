# Green Craft — Exterior geometry study

**Status:** Concept-art geometry transcription, revision 02; not an engineered vehicle.

## Public purpose

Green Craft records a monolithic, green, science-fiction craft exterior as a three-view visual study. The archived sheet interprets an illustration into top, side and forward-end views, identifying large visual elements and their relationships so later artists can keep the silhouette consistent.

It is a reference for appearance and design continuity. Relative proportions are *not* measurements of a real object. No flight system, structural material, interior space or propulsion mechanism is supplied by the sheet.

## Evidence limits

No full-scale dimensions, engineering CAD, manufactured hull, aerodynamic analysis, structural verification or flight evidence is established. Symmetry, roof relief and cross-view alignment are assumed for visualization. A distinctive exterior does not by itself imply a feasible spacecraft or aircraft.
