# Lifeguard Lance — Rescue drone and fleet

**Status:** Illustrated rescue concept; not available for emergency use.

## Public purpose

Imagine rapidly delivering flotation assistance near a swimmer while trained human rescuers respond.

## Evidence limits

No working flight-to-water device, certified buoyancy, swimmer-contact safety, reliable deployment, regulator approval or successful rescue testing is established.

## Next check

Seek professional lifeguard and rescue-equipment safety review before feasibility or operational claims.

## Credits and scope

Concept variants are held in Mr. Peloquin's collaborative archive. Independent engineering validation is unconfirmed. Existing rescue procedures must not be replaced by this proposal.
