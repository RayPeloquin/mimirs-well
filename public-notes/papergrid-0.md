# PAPERGRID-0 — One-roll energy-materials measurement concept

**Status:** Illustrated test-plan poster only; no fabricated test roll or measured output.

## Public purpose

PAPERGRID-0 narrows an ambitious roll-to-roll paper-based energy idea to an evidence-first question: can one short cartridge-sized material sample be made, activated, measured and responsibly recovered with its full inputs counted? The archive poster emphasizes pack-level observations and material accounting before extrapolating to a factory or grid installation.

The proposed public milestone is a reproducible laboratory record of the actual assembled sample, not a promotional estimate of how large the system might eventually become. The artwork depicts production, activation, measurement and recycling as a conceptual loop.

## Evidence limits

No functioning cell, measured energy density, useful cycle life, activation time, safe disposal, closed-loop material recovery, favorable net-energy balance or industrial scale-up is established. The poster mixes illustrative dimensions and numerical targets with untested assumptions; none should be treated as experimental results or a qualified electrical system.

## Next check

Commission an independent electrochemistry and materials-safety review to specify a small, contained, non-operational test plan and a complete input/output ledger. Resolve contradictory or unclear poster labels before using any of its figures in comparisons.
