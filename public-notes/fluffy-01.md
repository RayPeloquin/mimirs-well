# FLUFFY-01 — Canid-form observation robot

**Status:** Court-reviewed requirements concept, v0.1; unarmed, unbuilt and unqualified.

## Public purpose

FLUFFY-01 imagines an electrically powered quadruped with a removable, dire-wolf-inspired shell for supervised observation of terrain and conditions in authorized places. The animal-inspired appearance is an identity choice, not evidence of superior mobility or perception. The human operator would define and authorize the observation task; local control would remain constrained by the chosen platform's demonstrated limits.

The public concept expressly excludes pursuit, person identification, targeting and combat workflows. It distinguishes a command to stop from the robot's actual physical stopping response, and calls for transparent handling of uncertain location and permission.

## Evidence limits

No selected robot, built shell, measured sensor performance, completed hazard response, field mission, reliable stop envelope, military qualification or independent engineering approval is established. Four AI Court critiques identified requirements and failure modes but did not run an experiment or certify the machine. A canid rendering is not hardware.

## Next check

Choose an ordinary non-hazardous ground platform and a small observation task. Specify the operator's authority, test-environment limits, privacy controls, measured stop responses and failure records before any field trial. Benchmark mobility and observation separately.
