# SKIPJACK-LF — Low-hover logistics feasibility

**Status:** Preliminary engineering study, v0.1; model assumptions only, no aircraft.

## Public purpose

SKIPJACK-LF examines whether a purpose-built, uncrewed rotorcraft could move restrained cargo a short distance over difficult ground. The archived study intentionally narrows the initial use to an inert cargo carrier. A universal vehicle-lift attachment, passenger transport and a crewed craft are not established by the concept.

The source uses conventional rotorcraft momentum theory to make power, mass, energy and low-altitude recovery constraints visible. It also compares the idea with simpler alternatives such as ground movement and bridges rather than assuming that hover is always preferable.

## Evidence limits

No complete mass closure, supplier-confirmed power system, measured thrust or outwash, rotor-fault survival, regulatory flight permission, working prototype or successful flight test is established. Illustrative calculations in the archived brief depend on explicitly chosen inputs. The Court's critiques and arithmetic checks are not hardware validation or certification. A concept image is not an aeronautical drawing.

## Next check

First state a cargo-only use case and compare cost, risk and access with nonflight methods. Independently review mass, electrical power and failure behavior before considering restrained component tests; do not extrapolate to occupied vehicles.
