What does a vibration alert tell the person on watch?

The digest narrows an unusual-contact alert to a request for human review. Its cause remains open.

Public source review

What we intended

Use hull-borne vibration to draw attention to unusual contact or a possible maintenance issue.

What the source documents

The archived design describes advisory alerts. It supplies no calibrated hull trial, event-localization result or measured background false-alarm rate.

Possible crossover

Maintenance logs could retain the original observation alongside the later inspection finding.

Proposed next test

Compare alerts with independently logged ordinary disturbances on an inanimate fixture. Count quiet periods that triggered alerts as well as known events the system missed.
