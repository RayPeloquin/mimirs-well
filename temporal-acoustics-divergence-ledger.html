01 / The three questions

Temporal Acoustics · Divergence Record

01 / INTENDED

What we intended

Simulate controlled acoustic pulses through rock, reconstruct candidate cavities from receiver data, and challenge the method with cavity-free geology.

The saved v0.3 study uses five sources, thirteen receivers, and eight independently varied synthetic rock worlds.

02 / OBSERVED

What actually happened

In 32 held-out synthetic measurements (16 cavity-present, 16 absent), the calibrated interior-peak score reported 100% sensitivity and a 12.5% false-positive rate. But eight deliberately difficult solid inclusions triggered five false cavity alarms using the same frozen scoring rule.

The score is not a probability. Neither the small held-out set nor strong model AUC establishes real-world excavation or archaeological accuracy.

03 / CROSSOVER

What else might this be useful for?

A useful crossover is adversarial benchmarking for acoustic and geophysical classifiers: deliberately build 'not-the-target' conditions that mimic the target, then require evidence before assigning a physical interpretation.

Proposed methodology transfer, not validated real-world detection.

The divergence: A classifier that separates empty cavities from ordinary rock may still mistake solid high-contrast geology for empty space. The impostor belongs in the report, not in the footnotes.

03 / Next reproducible brick

How to test the next question

1 · Expand geology controls

Generate families of solid lenses, fractures, and variable attenuation under a fixed preregistered decision rule.

2 · Change measurement geometry

Model intersecting ray paths and multiple source/receiver depths instead of inferring deep structure from shallow direct arrivals alone.

3 · Test physical feasibility

Only after robust synthetic tests, assess permissions, instrument calibration, real-material ground truth, and non-invasive conservation constraints.

What should a later researcher not infer?

The deep-rock counterexample yielded essentially the same short-offset calibrated speed despite large differences in later waves. Direct arrivals at one shallow level do not uniquely determine the full deeper rock field. That limits claims about recovered layouts.
