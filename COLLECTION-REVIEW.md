# Mimir’s Well — Collection review

17 November 2026 · Review of published content and catalog structure

## Verdict and scope

The 70 expanded records make sense in a research/design collection **when read with their stated evidence and limitations**. They include proposed devices, computational studies, engineering audits, collaboration/review methods and creative work; they are not 70 demonstrated inventions. No expanded record was removed or promoted to validated status by this review.

The other 327 entries are retained as **archive titles awaiting assessment**, not endorsed designs. A title alone cannot establish a mechanism, feasibility or a reason to build it. They should not be presented as completed engineering. This register covers all 397 published entries and gives every entry a disposition.

This pass read all published titles and the purpose, evidence and limitations fields of the 70 expanded summaries. It checked record binding, exact-title duplication, required fields and presentation. It did not independently reopen every original research source, rerun experiments/models, establish novelty, perform clinical/physical qualification or audit every external link. Conclusions are about the public descriptions, not certification of their underlying research.

## Important boundaries retained

- Ghostglass is a synthetic statistical reference, not a working neutrino detector. SKEPTIC-1, CRUCIBLE, GRASP-01 and other numerical demonstrations remain model-specific.
- The Roman-concrete audit, SR-MARGIN-01 and guarded grasp results retain failed checks, adverse outcomes and limitations; an honest negative result belongs in the collection.
- ECHO-Rx/ECHO-M and medical/heart concepts do not establish biological or clinical capability. Public descriptions keep research and patient use separate.
- HIPPO water collection, solids pre-treatment, treatment verification, cooling and energy allocation remain distinct roles. No water-safety, energy-creation or deployed-performance claim is added.
- Court agreement, internal approval labels, a proposed permission gate and a written safety requirement are not independent validation or implemented enforcement.
- Transporter T0 concerns state transfer between stationary carriers; Temporal Reflection concerns a proposed switched transmission line. Their descriptions do not support matter transport or room-wide time manipulation.
- Private archived-source citations limit public reproducibility. A fully public, dated evidence package would strengthen a record, but unavailable downloads are not silently invented.
