01 / The three questions

BRIDLE-3 · Divergence Record

01 / INTENDED

What we intended

Explore six replaceable hot modules sharing a cooled frame, with optional thermal coupling, ideal switching contacts and local reserve cooling.

The reference model compares local loss against total loss over normalized model time; 1.00 is the initial normalized temperature rise, and 1.50 is a chosen model comparison limit.

02 / OBSERVED

What actually happened

With one affected module retaining 20% of main cooling, the modeled peak was 1.852 when weakly coupled and 1.453 when strongly joined. A local reserve reduced it to 1.205. But if all six modules lose main cooling, identical modules have no temperature gradient to share heat; the original reserve assumption approaches 2.102, beyond the chosen 1.50 limit.

A reserve sized only through normalized time 12 reaches the limit then later rises toward 1.529. All temperatures and time are dimensionless; no real engine heat capacity or coolant inventory was measured.

03 / CROSSOVER

What else might this be useful for?

The generalizable concept is failure-aware modular thermal analysis: shared heat paths help local faults only while cooler neighbors exist. Candidate crossovers include battery-module thermal buffering, industrial heater arrays and redundant electronics cooling.

These are potential systems-engineering analogies, not validated products.

The divergence: A design can pass at the end of its test window and fail during the heat soak afterward. Model the whole tail, not just the convenient stopping time.

03 / Next reproducible brick

How to test the next question

1 · Audit the thermal assumptions

Verify energy conservation, conductance coupling and switching logic; preserve the existing mathematical checks.

2 · Test the entire shutdown tail

Compare reserve schedules beyond the chosen time window and allow asymmetric faults, valve failures and finite stored coolant.

3 · Acquire physical parameters

Measure heat capacities, coolant delivery, joints and thermal gradients in a harmless benchtop rig before asserting engine benefit.

What should a later researcher not infer?

The reported 1.50 comparison value is a modeled study threshold, not a material limit or a hardware requirement. Time is scaled by heat capacity over cooling conductance. Even an ideal reserve must be integrated over subsequent residual heat.
